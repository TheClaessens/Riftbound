patch notes · 9 october 2026 · version 6.0

The Crownshard

The three Rift Obelisks are gone. Each land now has one Crownshard, a shard of the broken Riftcrown, at its heart. It wakes at 0:50. Press F beside it and time stops, so you can read your pacts side by side before you choose. Our own playtest showed that the obelisks were hard to find, and impossible to read and choose in the middle of a fight. The stakes and rewards are unchanged.

The Crownshard

The obelisks were three stones scattered across the map, and nothing said why a boss would come for them.

  • remove
    Rift Obelisks. The three obelisks are gone. Their pacts now live at the Crownshard.
  • new
    One shard in every land. It stands at the heart of the map, and you start each act right beside it. Until 0:50 it sleeps, chained and dim.
  • new
    It wakes at 0:50. The chains snap, the shard lifts into the air and its light column shows across the land. Press F within reach of it to commune.
  • new
    The boss is drawn to its light. When the boss comes, the shard rises up its column and leaves a cold ruin behind.

The Pact screen

Every choice is on one screen, so you can compare them before you commit.

  • new
    Time stops. When you press F, the world loses its colour and freezes until you close the screen.
  • new
    Pact of Echo is a switch: the boss comes back once more after you beat it.
  • new
    Pact of Wrath sets a heat from 0 to 3. Each step shows the boss affix it adds before you pick it, so you know what you are getting.
  • new
    Call it now seals your choice and brings the boss in 3 seconds. Seal the pact on its own and the boss still comes at 3:00. Walk away (Esc) changes nothing.
  • new
    Each pact has its own sigil and colour, and its name is always on the card.

The horde answers

The risk now comes after you commit, not while you are trying to read the choices.

  • change
    Each new pact you seal makes the horde answer. After a short flare, foes burst out around the shard, and new spawns for a few seconds gather on it.
  • change
    Call it now skips the answer: the boss comes in 3 seconds, and the rest of the act's horde goes with it.
  • new
    Come back any time. You can return to the shard until the boss arrives. Sealed pacts stay sealed. The pause gives you no shelter: you pick up exactly where you were.

Same stakes, same rewards

The bet itself was the part you enjoyed, so only how you reach it has changed.

  • change
    Pact payouts are unchanged. Shards, Echo Chests, Wrath Caches and boss relics are paid the same way, and only if you win the boss fight.

Sound and sight

  • new
    New sounds for the shard waking, time stopping, each pact sealed, the horde's answer and the shard rising. The music ducks under the Pact screen while a low drone plays.

Under the hood

  • change
    The game now ships its own copy of Three.js and its fonts, so it no longer depends on outside CDNs and starts faster.
  • change
    Arrow keys no longer scroll the page.

patch notes · 9 october 2026 · version 5.2

Raid sustain & targeting

Boss fights now give some health back, the adds that matter stand out, and auto-cast knows which one to shoot first. Break Checks read more clearly, gold now means only "go here", and every boss brings its minions into the fight. Playtesters' findings from round three shaped each change.

Play Riftbound
Health inside the fightMinion orbs, a top-up at each phase and a full dash in every intermission.
Auto-cast with prioritiesRunners, pods and banners come before the boss.
Gold means safeEverything to destroy has its own colour now.

Health in the fight

Nothing healed you inside a boss arena, so deaths often came after attrition with nothing to fall back on.

  • new
    Minions join the fight. Each phase, a small wave of the boss's minions appears at the arena wall. They give normal XP, and about one in twelve drops a health orb. Waves pause during a Break Check and never come during an intermission.
  • new
    Break Check reward. Breaking a Break Check sometimes drops a health orb at the boss's feet.
  • new
    Phase top-up. Clearing an intermission restores 12% of your maximum health.
  • change
    Full dash for every intermission. You start each intermission with all your dash charges, instead of getting one at the end.

Auto-cast with priorities

Auto-cast kept firing at the boss while its runners healed him, and it ignored the banners and pods that decide a fight.

  • change
    With a boss up, auto-cast picks targets in this order: runners and torchbearers, then Rift Crystals inside the boss's blast, then pods, banners, conduits and the wyvern, then minions, and the boss last. Outside boss fights it works as before.
  • new
    Marks on the adds that matter. Runners and torchbearers glow orange, banners cyan, spore pods lime, conduits violet and the wyvern pale cyan. Each has a ground ring, and most have a line to the boss. An arrow on the screen edge points to any that are off screen.

Gold means go here

Gold was also on things to destroy, which made the safe spots hard to tell apart.

  • change
    Conduits, the beam core, the storm ward, tempest strikes and spore pods are no longer gold. Gold stays on safe pockets, shelters, cleanse pools and the gaps in King's Wrath.
  • change
    The big XP gem is violet, and storm's rings are white-blue, so none of them look like safe ground.

Break Checks you can read

  • fix
    A Break Check target could read as a number above the top of the bar (171 on a 100 bar). It now shows as a percentage of the bar, such as break 60%.
  • change
    The target grows by at most 1.5 times within a phase, so a long phase no longer pushes it out of reach.

Intermissions you can survive

Several playtesters lost a lot of health in intermissions. These changes lower the damage without removing the danger.

  • change
    King's Wrath rings have a gold walk-through gap that follows your side of the arena. Ring damage is lower.
  • change
    Vashkar's lava lanes are thinner and hit softer. Each intermission has one safe gold pocket, and the whole intermission can take at most 30% of your maximum health.
  • change
    Aurelith's Prism Crash takes at most 22% of your maximum health.

Steadier fight lengths

Burst builds could skip a boss's script, and single-target builds ran into the soft enrage.

  • change
    Each phase lasts at least 12 seconds for Gorrak, 18 for Vashkar and 30 for Aurelith, counting fight time. Time spent Downed doesn't count.
  • change
    Boss health: Gorrak 12,000 (was 14,500) and Vashkar 21,500 (was 24,000).
  • change
    Vashkar's banners shield him 15% each (was 20%), and his soft enrage starts at 115 seconds (was 95).

Stagger rewards skill

Reactions filled most of the stagger bar, while dodges and crystal blasts barely moved it.

  • change
    A perfect dodge or a Rift Crystal blast on a boss now adds 20 Stagger, up from 10.
  • change
    Repeated reactions fade faster, so spamming one kind of reaction fills the bar more slowly.

Obelisks in plain words

Some card text used jargon, and one line seemed to contradict itself.

  • change
    Windfall, Strain and Heat are gone from the card text. Haste reads net −2 levels by the boss, Wrath is tagged Danger and says the boss gets harder, and the Echo card says you take 10% more damage.
  • change
    Haste warns you when your health is below 40%: Low HP: the boss arrives now.
  • change
    In Act III, Haste no longer pays a shard bounty, and its card leaves out the chest line.
  • new
    The Rift Mend is announced when it starts. You heal 20% and your dash refills before the Echo rises, and the Echo card lists it.

The death screen

The damage list covered the whole run, and the screen never showed what the fight had cost you.

  • new
    This fight. The damage list covers the boss fight you lost, not the whole run.
  • new
    Your bets. Shows the obelisks you armed, the health you entered the fight with, and the shards you lost.
  • fix
    The killer's row now matches the fight's damage breakdown.
  • new
    One-time tips for the phase top-up and the Rift Mend.

Patch notes in the game

  • new
    These notes now live next to the game. Open them from the scroll button on the title screen, beside Settings and Controls.
  • new
    Feedback button. The title screen and the pause menu now have a Feedback button. Pick Bug, Balance, Idea or Other, write a short note, and it opens a pre-filled GitHub issue (run details such as class, act and level are attached unless you turn them off). You need a free GitHub account; you can also copy the text and send it to the team.

The Rift Obelisks Update

You now decide when the boss comes and how hard it hits. Each boss fight is now a sealed, three-phase encounter with its own music, mechanics you can read and learn, and a stagger bar that rewards clever play. A panel of playtesters tried it first, and their findings shaped the final tuning below.

Play Riftbound
Call the boss your wayThree Rift Obelisks trade risk for rewards.
Raid-style bossesSealed arenas, three phases, intermissions.
A score per bossEach boss has its own music, which builds with the fight.

Rift Obelisks

Until now the boss arrived at 3:00, every time. That still happens if you do nothing.

  • new
    At 0:50 of every act, three obelisks rise from the ground. Walk up to one to read its card: what it costs, what it pays, and (for Haste) roughly how many levels you would skip.
  • new
    To activate an obelisk, stand in its ring for 4 seconds. The horde gathers around you while you do, so choosing risk is itself risky. If you step out, progress drains slowly instead of resetting.
  • new
    Rewards are paid only if you win the boss fight, and every card says so. In Act III, where the run ends at the kill, chest rewards turn into extra shards.

HasteSummons the boss now.The rest of the act's horde, about 2–4 levels.A burst of XP, shards for the time you saved, a boss chest with 4 choices instead of 3.

Boss encounters

  • new
    The arena seals. A wall of thorns, basalt or crystal rises around you, the horde dissolves into XP, and the boss lands. Level-ups wait until the boss is down on the ground, so the arrival is never interrupted.
  • new
    Three phases. At two-thirds and one-third health the boss starts an intermission: it can't be harmed while you survive an arena-wide pattern, then it is Winded and open to free hits.
  • new
    Stagger. Elemental reactions, freezes, stuns, burning, chilled or shocked status, perfect dodges and Rift Crystal blasts fill the boss's stagger bar. Fill it and the boss is Downed, taking 40% more damage. Single-element builds can stagger too.
  • new
    Break Checks. Once a phase, the boss starts a named cast such as War Horn. Fill the stagger bar to the marker before it finishes, or the cast goes off.
  • new
    Red means get out, striped gold means go here. Gold zones are cleanse pools, safe pockets and shelters behind pillars. Every boss attack shows on the ground first.
  • new
    Adds come only when a mechanic needs them: spore pods to shoot before they ripen, banners that shield their warchief, runners racing to heal their king, a wyvern that leaves a lightning rod behind.

The three bosses

  • change
    Gorrak, the Troll King: stand behind his backhand, shoot his spore pods for healing sap, break his War Horn, dodge the trees he hurls, and dash through King's Wrath.
  • change
    Vashkar, the Ashen Warchief: step out, then back in, for his axe combo. Topple his war banners before their torchbearers relight them. Don't double back over your cinder marks. The arena shrinks when the Molten Tide rises. His frame now shows Resists Fire 50%.
  • change
    Aurelith, the Prism Dragon: her ward colour tells you her next attack, crystal pillars block her beams, her conduits feed the ward, and in the last phase the river floods the arena.
  • new
    Music. Each boss has its own score that changes with phases, intermissions, staggers and enrage, and ends on a victory cadence: war drums and a bone horn for Gorrak, a brass march for Vashkar, organ and choir for Aurelith.

Rewards

  • new
    Six Boss Relics, offered only by Echo Chests and Wrath chests: Gorrak's Tusk, Vashkar's Brand, Aurelith's Scale, Riftbreaker's Sigil, Ashen Standard and Hollow Heartstone.
  • new
    A Wrath Cache at heat 2 or more: after the fight, add a rank to a skill of your choice.
  • change
    The passive called Haste is now Quickening, so it no longer shares a name with the obelisk.

Tuned after playtesting

Six playtesters fought these bosses before release. These changes come straight from what they hit.

  • fix
    King's Wrath rings came faster than some classes can dash, in an arena you can't run out of. They now come 3.2 seconds apart, as do Aurelith's frost rings.
  • fix
    Gorrak's follow-up backhand after a slam couldn't be dodged without a dash. It now gives more warning and lunges less.
  • fix
    Chained freezes could keep a boss frozen for the whole fight. Bosses now shrug off a new freeze for 4 seconds after one, and can be stunned at most every 1.5 seconds.
  • change
    Reaction-heavy builds kept bosses Downed almost permanently. Repeated reactions now fill the stagger bar less, while perfect dodges and crystal blasts count double. Stagger you've built up now carries into a Break Check instead of resetting.
  • change
    Fights ran long. Boss health is retuned, and Vashkar's banners shield him less (20% each).
  • new
    The death screen now names the exact move that killed you, with a tip for that move. One-time tips explain stagger, intermissions, Winded and King's Wrath.
  • change
    Clearer fights: banners no longer stack on top of each other, the boss frame is smaller, trees fade when they hide the boss, and the Haste obelisk is now pale cyan so gold only ever means "go here".

The Clear Sight Update

We watched a panel of playtesters run the Rift and listened to what tripped them up. This update fixes an unwinnable final boss, makes your hero easy to find in the thickest fights, and finally tells you what killed you.

Play Riftbound
Aurelith can fallPrism-Shamans no longer shield the final boss.
Never lose your heroYour hero shows through your own spells.
Know your killerA new death recap shows what hit you, and how.

Bosses & bugs

  • fix
    Aurelith, the Prism Dragon could become impossible to damage while Kobold Prism-Shamans were nearby, because their shields also covered the boss. Shamans no longer shield bosses, so the final fight can be won again.
  • new
    When the act boss is off screen, a red skull marker on the screen edge points to it and shows how far away it is.
  • fix
    Reaction echoes (from Prism Lens and similar relics) could set off further echoes, which pushed your Chain into the thousands. Now only reactions you cause can echo.
  • fix
    Arcanist glyphs could stay frozen on screen at the start of your next run.
  • fix
    The reaction feed on the right no longer piles up stale rows during long fights.

Seeing the fight

  • new
    Your hero stays visible. When your own clouds, explosions or glyphs cover you, your hero's colours come back and a thin outline in your class colour traces them. The ring under your feet always shows. In calm moments nothing changes.
  • change
    In busy fights, your own spell effects fade slightly right around your hero. Enemy projectiles and red warning zones are never faded.
  • change
    Low health no longer drains the colour from the whole screen. The edges pulse red and your hero gets a pulsing red outline, so you can still see the danger you need to escape.
  • change
    Fissure Geysers (Act II): the cracks now glow red-orange inside the red warning ring, then cool to dark embers after they erupt, so a spent geyser no longer looks like a new one.
  • change
    Big numbers are shortened: the Chain counter reads ×3k instead of ×2993.

Fairer damage

Several testers lost half their health to one hit in Act II and never saw it coming.

  • change
    No single hit can take more than 30% of your maximum health.
  • change
    War Boars and Crag Drakes no longer charge all at once. Each charge starts at least 0.6 seconds after the last, so you can dodge them one by one.
  • new
    Death recap. When you fall, the end screen shows what finished you (Slain by War Boar · charge · 41), your three biggest sources of damage, your last few hits with your health after each, and one tip for next time.

Classes

  • change
    Arcanist: every third Arcane Bolt volley is Prismatic and carries Fire, then Storm, then Frost. The Arcanist can now set up its own Combust, Arc Flash and Shatter from the first minute, without other elements.
  • change
    Cryomancer: Frost Armor now absorbs 60% of a hit instead of all of it. Still the sturdiest class, but no longer untouchable.
  • change
    Unlock order. The Cryomancer now unlocks first (defeat Gorrak, the Troll King). The Arcanist unlocks once you discover 4 reactions. Classes you have already unlocked stay unlocked.

Levelling & drafting

  • new
    Several level-ups at once (after a boss, for example) are now picked one after another in a single window, marked 1 of 3, 2 of 3, instead of the window closing and reopening each time.
  • change
    Smoother early levels. The XP needed no longer jumps sharply at level 5, which used to leave you stuck around level 7 for over a minute.
  • change
    Better hands. Every level-up now offers at least one card that grows your build: a new skill, a rank-up or a fusion. No more hands of only passives. You'll be offered a skill of your class's element by level 3.
  • change
    Banish now removes a skill for the rest of the run, including its rank-ups and any fusion hints that need it.
  • fix
    Fusion hints were misleading. Each fusion needs a specific pair of skills, not any two at rank 3. Cards now name the partner and its rank (Fusion path: Blizzard r2/3 → Thunderhead), fusion cards say they replace both skills, and a ready fusion tells you it arrives at your next level-up.

Rift Shards

  • change
    Your best Chain now adds at most 30 shards to a run's reward. Very long chains used to pay hundreds, which made one build the only sensible way to earn. Kills, acts reached, bosses and Rift Tier pay as before. Chain's in-run bonuses are unchanged.
  • new
    Abandoning a run now tells you what you would give up. The confirm button reads Forfeit 50 shards?, because an abandoned run pays nothing.
The Act II hint now says it plainly: red rings with glowing cracks erupt as geysers, so step out. Thanks to everyone who played. Keep telling us what trips you up.
