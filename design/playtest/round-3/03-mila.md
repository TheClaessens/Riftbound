# Mila — cozy casual · playtest round 3
**Verdict (in character).** It is gorgeous and the Seal is a real "ooh" moment: a hundred goblins melt into sparkles, the lavender flash wipes the screen, the thorn/lava wall closes and a giant lands. But the fight that follows is either over before I got excited (Gorrak in 37 s with my fire build) or a 3-minute marathon (Gorrak in 177 s with the ice build), and both times I died in Act II to Vashkar's floor fire and a charge I never saw coming, with the Orc Shamans having chewed my HP beforehand. I'd come back tomorrow, mostly for the horde part and to see the next boss.
Fun: 7.5/10 · Would play again tomorrow: yes · Domain score (epic feel / intensity / frustration): 6.5/10

Persona note: casual hands, fresh save, primer on, I read no design doc. I picked cards by what sounded cool (a small helper in the page did the picking from my taste rules; every pick is in the log below). The Cryomancer run is where I picked Glass Sigil ("Beautiful. Fragile. Lethal."), which I would honestly do as Mila; it cost me 40 % max HP and I think it contributed to the death.

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **The Seal is a genuine event.** In four consecutive 1.2 s steps around 3:00 I saw: a packed horde (~100 foes) and a red-circle-strewn screen, then the lavender/white flash with the horde dissolving into blue XP sparkles, then a ring of thorns (Act I) or glowing lava rock (Act II) closing around me, then the boss dropping in with an orange streak. It changes the whole mood in about 3 seconds. [seen] (shots: `playtest/out/mila-r3-c2-peak-horde.png`, `mila-r3-c2-seal1.png`, `mila-r3-c2-seal2.png`, `mila-r3-a-gseal2.png`, `mila-r3-a-gseal3.png`)
- **Horde chaos is the best-looking part of the game.** Meteors, Singularity vortices, Spirit Orbs, chain numbers in the hundreds (x909, x910): "I feel strong". [seen] (shots: `mila-r3-horde-chaos.png`, `mila-r3-act2-horde.png`, `mila-r3-c2-peak-horde.png`)
- **The two bosses look distinct.** Gorrak is a mossy troll with a club and green spore pods, in a thorn ring; Vashkar is a horned warchief with glowing lava lanes, banners with teal beams, and an ember-coloured arena. Different palettes, different attacks. [seen] (shots: `mila-r3-seal-arrival.png`, `mila-r3-a-vashkar-lava.png`, `mila-r3-vashkar-mid.png`)
- **Telegraphs are readable and pretty.** Red circles/rectangles on the ground, banner beams, the intermission bubble around Gorrak, the dimmed screen at low HP. I could read "danger here" in every shot. [seen] (shots: `mila-r3-boss-midfight.png`, `mila-r3-c-gorrak-mid.png`, `mila-r3-vashkar-mid.png`)
- **Death screen names the move and gives one useful sentence.** "Slain by Vashkar cinder mark ... next time Cinder marks drop where you walk, then explode in order. Keep moving, and never double back." / "Banner charge sends Vashkar along a red line. Step sideways." / "Vashkar leaves burning ground behind him. Don't stand in the flames." Also shows "The last moments: 4 hits in 1.8 s". I understood *what* hit me every time. [read]
- **One-time tips land.** Stagger ("fill it to knock the boss DOWN: it takes +40 % damage"), Winded ("free hits after the intermission"), and the seal tip ("Red = get out, striped gold = go here. Every attack is shown on the ground first") appeared at the moment I needed them and were short. [read]
- **The obelisk card is clear when you stand next to it.** "THE BOSS COMES TWICE / Strain: +10 % damage taken / Echo Chest: pick 1 of 3 Boss Relics / Paid only if you win the boss fight" and the line "Doing nothing is fine" in the first-appearance tip took the fear away. [read]
- **Level-ups are held until the boss has landed**, so the entry is not hidden behind a modal. [data] (decisions arrived 2 s after the landing in all three runs)
- **Pre-boss relic/health refills are generous.** HP was back to full at the start of Act II in every run. [data]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **Boss fight length depends wildly on the build, so "epic" is hit-or-miss.** Gorrak took 37 s (Pyromancer), 42 s (Arcanist) and 177 s (Cryomancer). At 37 s he never got past phase 2 before melting; I barely saw the Troll King do anything but swing twice. At 177 s I saw the whole kit, three War Horn checks, an enrage at about 2:14 into the fight, and I was at 18/146 HP at 7 % boss HP; exciting at the end, but the middle was a slog. Intensity should be roughly the same for every class; right now the class decides whether the boss is a speed bump or a marathon. [data] · Severity: major
2. **I died inside Vashkar's first intermission, when I could not hurt him anyway.** Boss at 66 %, 4 lava hits in 1.8 s, dead. In an intermission there is no payoff for me (he is untargetable), so dying there feels like pure loss. Lava lanes plus "ground fire" (45 + 11 + 24 damage) were the bulk of the loss in two of three runs. [data] (`mila-r3-c2-vashkar-mid.png`, `mila-r3-death.png`) · Severity: major
3. **HP attrition comes from the horde before the Seal, then I carry it into a fight with no real healing.** Orc Spirit-Shaman projectiles were 56 % / 24 % / 23 % of my total damage across the runs, ahead of any boss move. In run 3 I walked into the Seal at 84/132 after taking 78 damage from shamans in the last 25 s, then lost 78 to lava and 40 to the banner charge. The death screen says "Slain by Vashkar" but the damage breakdown says shamans did the most; that is honest but I would feel cheated. [data] · Severity: major
4. **Counting down the chain to zero feels like losing my power.** In the horde my chain was x500 to x900; the instant the arena sealed it fell to 0 and rarely got above 40 during the fight, because there is only one target. The big number is my "I'm strong" feedback and the boss fight turns it off. [data] (chain 903 at 6:58, chain 0 at 7:19) · Severity: minor
5. **Intermissions are a lull.** Eight to nine seconds of an untargetable boss in a bubble, with a "survive" pattern I can barely see (a few small mid-air telegraphs). After the horde's wall of noise it felt like the game paused on purpose. Fine once; the second one in a fight felt like a chore. [seen] (`mila-r3-intermission.png`, `mila-r3-intermission2.png`) · Severity: minor
6. **The boss is small in the frame, the landing is brief.** The boss drops in from the edge of the screen and, between the flash and the first red circle, I mostly noticed my own sparkles. A beam of light and a streak announce him, but I would love one slow beat where the camera frames him. [seen] (`mila-r3-seal-arrival.png`, `mila-r3-a-gseal3.png`) · Severity: polish
7. **Glass Sigil's flavour text lures cozy players into a trap.** "Beautiful. Fragile. Lethal." tagged "risk" is exactly what Mila would pick; −40 % max HP (146 to 88) then made the intermission lava a one-way trip. The description is honest; I just think the first relic offers should not contain a build-wrecker for a new player. [data] · Severity: minor
8. **Obelisks are inviting to look at, hard to use with casual hands.** Three attempts to channel Haste and two to channel Echo from afar failed: my character hovered 4 to 8 units away from the ring and never held 4 s while goblins ran in. It worked once, when Echo was close (distance 19, then d=2). This is probably the bot (casual hands) and not the game, but a casual human also has to walk to a ring with the horde on their heels. [data] · Severity: minor / [harness]

## Bugs & oddities
- After `PT.start` with a fresh save the first Pyromancer run reported `assist: 1` (no assist), fine; but the end-screen shard count shows "+0" because rAF is frozen (known harness caveat; `PT.end()` numbers are right).
- The Haste card text changed live as the act clock advanced: "Skips 130 s / ≈ −2 levels" at 1:00, "Skips 76 s / ≈ −1 level" at 1:43. Nice, but the payout also fell (+356 XP to +236 XP, ≈ +33 to ≈ +19 shards), so "waiting" quietly makes the obelisk worse. A new player may not notice they are losing value by thinking.
- Run 1, Act II start: for about 30 s I sat at level 11 with 1090 → 1326 kills and only +7 % XP; the level curve after Gorrak felt stalled before the Act II relic and Elite Surge.
- Haste's cost line "Skips 125 s of horde (Elite Surge, Swarm)" reads as a penalty; I never understood from the card alone that the horde-skip is the *payout* (bigger Windfall, 4-relic chest) and the "−1 level" is the cost.
- Cryomancer run: "Gorrak's War Horn" was NOT broken twice in a row (stagger target 60 then 117 with my bar at 0), then broken (72, 70, 171). The thresholds jump a lot (60, 117, 72, 70, 171), which is hard to read as a player.

## Domain deep-dive
**Does the boss arrival feel like an event now?** Mostly yes. The sequence (horde melts to XP sparkles, white-lavender flash, ring of thorns or glowing lava rocks, the boss drops in with an orange streak) is the clearest "now it's serious" cue in the game, and the ANNOUNCE banner ("GORRAK, THE TROLL KING. The old king of the Thornwood wakes.") lands well. What diminishes it: the camera never frames the boss, I always have 2 to 3 level-up modals back to back right after the landing (levels 18 and 19 at 9:03, then 19 again), and the boss sits at the edge of the screen when the first red circle appears. So "yes, event" for the Seal itself; "less so" for the boss's entrance proper. [seen]

**Is the boss fight more or less exciting than the horde part before it?** Different, not strictly more or less. The horde is the big dopamine: 100 enemies, x900 chain, Meteors and vortices, nothing can really stop me (HP stayed 90/90 for the entire first 2:30 of run 1). The boss is more *tense* and less *powerful*. When it was 177 s (Cryomancer Gorrak) the climax was better than any horde moment: 18 HP, boss at 7 %, a War Horn break at the last second, "he falls". When it was 37 s (Pyromancer Gorrak) it was less exciting than the preceding horde because I never felt in danger and the boss never got to do anything. Vashkar was always exciting, because lava lanes, banners, charges and the cinder marks fill the arena with red, but exciting in a "stressful" way I cannot control as a casual (the bot dodged only some of it). [seen/data]

**How the intensity changes when the Seal closes — what I pay attention to.**
Before the Seal I look at the crowd: where are the crystals, which side is thicker, my chain number, XP bar, picking relics. I am an artist painting the screen with spells. The camera is full of things, no single thing matters. I rarely look at HP because it hardly moves.
During the fight I look at the *ground* around my feet: red circles and rectangles, the cast bar (War Horn: "stagger to 70 in 5 s"), the stagger bar, the HP bar, and I start to count seconds. The screen is nearly empty (1 to 4 other enemies), so every red shape is a dare. I stop looking at the numbers and watch two things: the boss and the floor. My spells still pretty, but their job is now "fill the stagger bar" rather than "kill things".
Did I like the change? I liked it a lot in principle: I need the horde to be mindless and the boss to be tense. I did not like (a) the chain going to 0, (b) intermissions where the screen is quiet and I can do nothing, and (c) when the tension comes from floor fire that I cannot read well in the middle of my own particle effects. [seen/read]

**When I died, did I understand why? Did I want to try again?**
Yes to both, but the answers are different per death.
- Run 1: "Vashkar cinder mark": clear, because the death screen told me exactly ("Cinder marks drop where you walk, then explode in order. Keep moving, and never double back.") and the death screen showed "2 hits in 0.7 s". I wanted to retry: yes, boss at 46 %.
- Run 2: "Vashkar lava" during an intermission: I understood the move but not why I was standing in it, and it felt unfair because the boss was untargetable. Retry: yes, because I was in the *intermission* and had a decent shot.
- Run 3: "Vashkar banner charge, one hit for 40 at 17 HP": understood, "step sideways off the red line" is a good hint, but the real cause of death was shaman attrition plus lava earlier. The death screen's top damage source was "Orc Spirit-Shaman 23 %", not Vashkar. That is the only time I thought "the game didn't tell me the real reason".
I wanted to try again each time (all three were within reach of the boss's last phase), which is the strongest sign the structure works. Three deaths at the same boss does start to wear though: the third time I was thinking "Vashkar again".

**Best moment.** The Cryomancer's last 15 seconds against Gorrak: 18/146 HP, boss at 7 %, an ENRAGED Gorrak, a War Horn break check that needed "stagger 171 in 5 s", and I got it ("War Horn BROKEN", boss DOWNED for 8 s, then "THE TROLL KING FALLS" at 21 HP). Runner-up: the first Seal on Vashkar, with the whole screen going from 100 foes to a quiet, glowing arena in one second. [seen/data] (`mila-r3-c-enraged.png`, `mila-r3-c2-seal2.png`)

**Worst moment.** Dying in Vashkar's first intermission on the Cryomancer run: the boss was untargetable, I had taken lava hits for 4 s in a row and the game had nothing to give me back. Runner-up: the Pyromancer's Gorrak dying in 37 s with me at full HP, which felt like a skipped boss. [data] (`mila-r3-c2-vashkar-mid.png`)

**Obelisks: inviting or scary?** Inviting to look at (tall glowing pillars in three colours, the tooltip says "Doing nothing is fine"), a bit scary to read. "Haste: ≈ −1 LEVEL" and "Skips 125 s of horde" sound like a punishment; "Echo: THE BOSS COMES TWICE" sounds awesome but "+10 % damage taken" and "Strain" sound like a debuff; "Wrath: Boss affix: Rift Storm" sounds like pure risk. As a cozy player I would take Echo (epic fantasy, the card lists a prize) and nothing else. I took Echo in run 3 (the bot managed to get it) and it never paid because I died in phase 2 of Vashkar, with the "Echo" never seen. [read/data]

**Round-3 changes that landed for me.**
- *Level-ups wait for the boss to land*: yes, good.
- *Death screen names the killing move*: yes, best change in the build for a casual.
- *One-time tips explaining Stagger, Winded, Break Check*: yes, short enough that I actually read them.
- *Gold = go here*: in screenshots the safe zones were pale yellow dashed circles around crystals/pools; I could not tell from a still whether they were "safe". (My own character was in one at one point and took no damage, so probably fine.)
- *Stagger from status*: my Cryomancer run (chill/freeze/superconduct: stagger gained 98 + 125 from superconduct alone) filled the bar without me caring, but the Pyromancer's Combust filled it too. So yes, a mono-element build can stagger.
- *Fight length*: Pyromancer 37 s, Arcanist 42 s, Cryomancer 177 s (the round-3 sims say Gorrak mean 68 s, so mine are the two extremes).

## Harness caveats
- Casual hands are poor at dodging ground fire and failed to hold obelisk rings 5 out of 6 times, so "obelisks are hard to use" and "Vashkar's lava kills me" are probably more severe than for a human. I give the *readability* verdicts more weight than the *survival* verdicts.
- My card choices came from a helper function that followed my taste rules (fusion first, new skill over upgrade, flashy names, risk tags down-weighted after run 2). That produced some spread-out builds (many skills at rank 1 to 3) that a thoughtful human would have focused; the Cryomancer's 177 s Gorrak is partly that.
- The screenshots have no HUD, so I could not judge the boss frame; I used `PT.ui()` text for stagger/cast.
- Shots are frozen frames; I cannot judge animation, camera shake or audio. The Seal "wash" and the boss drop are inferred from consecutive 1.2 s steps.
- Shard counts on the end screen read "+0" (rAF frozen); I used `PT.end()`.
- I did not use assist or jump; I never saw Aurelith or any Wrath channel.
