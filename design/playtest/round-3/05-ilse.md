# Ilse — comfort & readability · playtest round 3
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).** The raid fights are much easier to read than I feared: every attack is drawn on the ground before it lands, "go here" is a striped gold shape, and the break-check / intermission strips tell me what to do in words. I can still lose things, though: the small hero disappears under my own frost nova at close range, the runners and the wyvern are tiny and look like ordinary horde units, and **if I could not tell red from green** (I simulated a deutan filter over the live game) the red danger fills nearly vanish into the grass while yellow things (beam core, conduit tether, storm-crystal ring, pods) look exactly like the safe gold stripes. Good bones, one real accessibility gap, and a handful of "stuff in front of my hero" annoyances.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: yes · Domain score (readability of the raid fights): 7/10 (9/10 for normal colour vision, 4/10 under a simulated red-green deficiency)

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **Telegraph vocabulary is consistent and legible in normal colour.** Filled red cones/circles/lanes for hits (Overswing cone, Gorrak slam disc, Timberfall lanes, Aurelith sector fills), thick red band for shockwave rings (King's Wrath), solid pink-red beam after a thin red line. I could always tell where not to stand. [seen] (shots: `playtest/out/ilse-r3-vsw3.png`, `ilse-r3-gatk1.png`, `ilse-r3-gseq3.png`, `ilse-r3-kw3.png`, `ilse-r3-aatk2b.png`)
- **Gold stripes mean "go here", and in the fights I saw that is true.** Aurelith's pillar shadows, the Skyward intermission wedges, the safe quadrant in phase 2/3 and Gorrak's cleanse disc are all diagonal-striped gold; the pattern (not just the colour) makes them unmistakable, and the safe quadrant sitting next to three red-outlined ones teaches the rule in one glance. [seen] (shots: `ilse-r3-ainter1.png`, `ilse-r3-aobj4.png`, `ilse-r3-a3p3-4.png`, `ilse-r3-gphase2.png`)
- **Words next to the pictures.** The cast strip under the boss frame ("MOLTEN OVERSWING · BREAK IT — STAGGER 60 · 4.4"), the intermission strip ("INTERMISSION · I · TIMBERFALL · 4.6", "It cannot be harmed. Survive."), the floating "War Banner 31%" / "Prism Conduit 22m" labels, the "Prism Ward: Frost" caption and the phase title cards ("PHASE II · SPORE ROT") mean nobody has to decode colour alone. [seen/read] (shots: HUD screenshots at Gorrak War Horn, Vashkar Overswing, Aurelith landing)
- **Banners are the best-marked add.** Teal tether line from boss to banner, ring at the banner foot, icon + name + remaining shield. Even when the banner sits under a tree it reads. [seen] (`ilse-r3-vash-banners.png`)
- **Molten Tide is a model "shrinking arena" readout:** a heavy dark-brown ring with bright orange veins; dark on pale sand, so it also survives the colour-blind filter. [seen] (`ilse-r3-vp3-7.png`)
- **Aurelith's flood** turns the edge of the arena into obviously different (bright cyan, streaked) terrain; island shrink is clear. [seen] (`ilse-r3-a3p3-4.png`)
- **Occlusion dithering works:** when I or the boss stood behind the Gorrak thicket the hedge went see-through instead of hiding us. [seen] (`ilse-r3-gorrak-fight1.png`)
- **Death screen** names the killing move ("Slain by Vashkar · GROUND FIRE · 9"), lists the last five hits with HP and puts a "next time" line under it. Readable, good size, dark background. [seen/read]
- **Tips are short and in plain words** (Stagger, Break Check, Intermission, WINDED, King's Wrath). One idea per tip. [read]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **Red-green dependence: danger fills nearly disappear on grass for a deutan player.** With my deuteranopia filter on, Gorrak's Timberfall lanes turned into a faint outlined rectangle the same hue as the grass, his slam/sap-pool circles became olive-brown, and Vashkar's red ring telegraph was *less* visible than the harmless yellow ring of a storm crystal next to it. The red HP bar in the boss frame goes grey. Fills are semi-transparent mid-luminance red on mid-luminance green, so there is little brightness difference to fall back on. In Act II/III (sand, bright green islands) it is better but still hue-dependent. [seen under simulated CVD] (`ilse-r3-gseq3.png` vs the filtered pane screenshots of Timberfall, Overswing ring) · Severity: **major**
2. **Yellow/gold collisions weaken "gold = safe", especially under CVD.** Things that are *not* safe but yellow/gold: Aurelith's conduit tether line and conduit halo, the bright core of Aurelith's beam (turns pale yellow under the filter, same as the gold shelter stripes), storm-crystal dashed yellow rings, Stormcaller's yellow hero halo, ripe/unripe spore pods (pale yellow eggs), and Aurelith's own body when the ward tint is gold. In the filtered frame of the beam, "danger beam" and "safe pillar shadow" are the same yellow and only the diagonal stripes tell them apart. In normal colour it is fine because the stripes + saturation differ. [seen] (`ilse-r3-aatk2b.png`, `ilse-r3-aobj15.png`, `ilse-r3-landing`) · Severity: **major** for CVD players, minor otherwise
3. **Runners (Gorrak P3) and the wyvern are nearly invisible.** The runners racing to heal Gorrak are just Goblin Grunts: ~15–30 px, olive/brown on grass or the dark thicket, no ring, tether, arrow or name label, and they look exactly like the horde grunts that also appear. I noticed them only because boss HP rose (24 → 27 %, 14 → 17 %) after the wave. The Aurelith wyvern is a small red-orange figure that sits at the arena edge and is easy to miss in the frame. [seen] (`ilse-r3-grun10.png`, `ilse-r3-grun29.png`, `ilse-r3-aobj15.png`) · Severity: **major** (these adds exist to be prioritised)
4. **My hero vanishes inside my own effects when the boss is close.** Frost Nova + chill puff + boss freeze shell + damage numbers stack on top of a ~35 px hero whose only marker is a thin cyan ring. In `vatk3` I could not find the hero without hunting for the ring; in the Act III peak (160 foes, `a3peak`) the cyan ring is the only reliable cue and sits in white spray. The same cyan is used for banner rings, so the ring does not even uniquely mean "me". [seen] (`ilse-r3-vatk3.png`, `ilse-r3-a3peak.png`, `ilse-r3-a3peak-crop.png`) · Severity: **major**
5. **Overlays land on the arena exactly where I am looking.** (a) The one-time tip box is a big rectangle on the left-middle of the screen and covered the start of Gorrak's Timberfall lane and a nova effect. (b) The centred "INTERMISSION / Phase II" banners sit over the hero and the boss. (c) In the Act III peak the **Obelisk of Wrath approach card** popped up right beside my hero (≈25 % of the screen) with a 160-foe horde on screen. (d) Reaction feed (5 stacked chips + "+42 % dmg" bar) on the right and a "Relic discovered / New reaction" toast bottom-right stack up while a boss fight is running. (e) The boss frame is wide (~55 % of width) and objects at the top of the arena (banners, conduits) slide under it. Together they do not hide the hero but they do hide telegraphs. [seen] (`ilse-r3-ginter` HUD frames, `ilse-r3-a3peak` HUD frame) · Severity: **minor/major** (major only in the peak case)
6. **The big white "cloud puff" above the boss.** In almost every shot there is a large cartoon cloud on the boss's head (freeze/hit feedback?). It is the brightest, largest shape in the frame, hides the boss's head/silhouette and is bigger than the hero. Combined with the frozen boss turning white-blue (Gorrak and Vashkar look like generic ice blobs; Vashkar's DOWNED pose reads as a crouching beast, not "Vashkar"), boss identity is lost during Downed. [seen] (`ilse-r3-vp2-0.png`, `ilse-r3-gorrak-warhorn.png`) · Severity: **minor**
7. **Death advice is wrong for contact deaths.** Two of my three Vashkar deaths were "contact" hits (30 dmg each, 2 hits in 3.6 s; and 5 hits of 4–10). The "next time" line says "Vashkar's attacks show on the ground first. Read the red, and move before it lands." — contact hits have no ground telegraph, so the advice is misleading. [read] · Severity: **minor**
8. **Floating damage text collides with itself and with labels** ("SUPEI249NDUCT"; reaction name overprinted by damage number over the boss). At the peak, the damage-number cloud over the boss is unreadable. [seen] (`ilse-r3-genrage`, Gorrak HUD screenshot) · Severity: **polish**
9. **Break Check numbers don't match the bar.** The cast strip asks for "stagger 171 / 124 / 109 / 87 / 84" while the Stagger bar is drawn as 0–100 %, and the numbers change per cast. I understand "fill the bar" but not why the threshold marker shows a number above 100. [read] · Severity: **polish**

## Bugs & oddities
- **Break-check threshold units** (see pain point 9): `state.boss` read "casting War Horn (break check: stagger 171)" while the bar shows percent.
- **Gorrak sap pools from phase 2 stay on the ground into phase 3 and through both intermissions** (3–4 olive discs with red rims, plus new pods in P3). Not a bug maybe, but it adds a lot of static red-rimmed circles to a screen that also uses red for live telegraphs; hard to tell "old pool" from "new slam" at a glance. [seen] (`ilse-r3-kw3.png`, `ilse-r3-gphase3`)
- **Phase 3 Gorrak heals visibly in the HP bar with no feedback** (runners reach him; the bar creeps up). No flash, no number, no sound I could judge. [data] boss 23 % → 25 % and 24 % → 27 % while enraged.
- **Gorrak natural fight (Cryomancer, average hands) took 189 s vs the 60–75 s target;** Vashkar with Cryomancer/Stormcaller and *assist* never got past ~47 % in 5+ minutes while banners were up (1 % per 8 s in phase 1), but the Arcanist killed him in 96 s and the Pyromancer killed Gorrak in 56 s. Large build-to-build gap, not a readability item. [data, jumped/assisted]
- `PT.shot` takes ~1.1 s per frame (readback); not a game problem.
- Death screen on a jumped run shows "foes slain 0" and "+0 shards" (jump artefact / animated count, per the brief).
- Not seen: Vashkar's **Cinder Memory** marks (every Vashkar fight I reached phase 3 in ended before they appeared, or I died first) and the **ground-fire patches** after Overswing (I only saw their damage in `dmgBy`, not a clean frame). Intermission 2 of Aurelith passed between two calls.

## Domain deep-dive
**Round-3 questions for Ilse, answered:**

1. **Can I always find my hero?** *Mostly, not always.* In Gorrak's arena (open green, few effects) yes. In Vashkar's arena the cyan ring was easy against sand but Cryomancer's frost nova + chilled-boss shell hid it (`ilse-r3-vatk3`). In the Act III peak, with ~160 foes plus a frost nova on me, the only cue is a thin cyan ring; the figure is ~35 px tall. Stormcaller's yellow halo is easier against green but is yellow (see gold). Hero found without effort in 70 % of the frames I looked at, with effort in the rest. [seen]
2. **Can I find the boss?** Gorrak and Vashkar: yes, they are big and have a named frame. Weak points: (a) the white puff on the boss's head; (b) when frozen/Downed they look like generic ice blobs; (c) Aurelith at the arena edge is small and her tint (gold/orange/white by ward) changes. The "Prism Ward: Frost" caption helps. [seen]
3. **Adds: easy to spot and easy to tell apart from boss attacks?**
   - *Spore pods:* easy (pale egg + green ring + glow), clearly an object and not an attack; their transformation into red-rimmed sap pools is a good "this is now danger" signal. Under CVD the pod's yellow resembles gold, but the egg shape and ring save it. [seen]
   - *War Banners:* best of the set (tether, ring, icon, label with shield %). [seen]
   - *Runners (Gorrak P3):* **poor.** They are plain Goblin Grunts, small and camouflaged on grass/thicket, no marker, no tether, same sprite as horde grunts. I identified them only from the HP bar rising. [seen]
   - *Prism Conduits:* medium. Gold crystal on a pedestal with a gold halo and a gold tether to the boss plus a floating "Prism Conduit 22m" label; fine in normal vision but visually part of the gold family and drowned by the many other crystals (pillars, rift crystals, arena wall) in Aurelith's arena. [seen]
   - *Wyvern:* **poor** at this zoom: ~40 px red figure at the arena rim; easy to mistake for a decoration or leftover horde. The "lightning rod" it leaves is a small gold-ish marker (I never got a clean frame of it). [seen]
   - Adds vs boss attacks: attacks are drawn as flat red shapes *on the ground*; adds are standing objects/units with rings and labels. That split works. The one confusion: red-rimmed sap pools (persistent) look like red-rimmed slam discs (momentary); the pools' olive fill and spore puffs help, but only when you look at them.
4. **Is gold now reserved for "safe"?** Within the *safe-zone system* yes (stripes + gold, consistent in all three acts). Across the whole screen no: see pain point 2 (conduit tether/halo, beam core under the filter, storm-crystal ring, Stormcaller halo, pods, Aurelith body tint, the obelisk ring card in gold). Under a deutan filter, the beam and the shelter stripes are the same yellow. [seen]
5. **Red that isn't danger?** Vashkar's arena wall (glowing orange rim), the fire-crystal's orange ring, Gorrak's phase-1 hero "hit" tint on me, and Aurelith's red-orange wyvern/dragon body; the pink-red enemy projectile orbs *are* danger. None of these were confusing in normal colour; the arena rim (orange) is the one I hesitated over (is it lava?). Vashkar's lava trails (brown with orange crackle) are correctly "danger" and survive CVD thanks to dark-vs-pale contrast. [seen]
6. **Boss frame readable? Covers the arena?** The frame (name, phase pips, HP bar, Stagger bar with marker, AXE HEAT, resist chip, state chips ENRAGED/DOWNED/SEALED/WINDED) is clear and well laid out. State chips are small and dim (the ENRAGED chip is the one I'd miss). It is ~55 % of screen width x ~14 % height at top-centre. Because the camera follows the hero, the hero is never under it, but banners/conduits near the top edge slide under it. The cast strip below it ("MOLTEN OVERSWING · BREAK IT — STAGGER 60 · 4.4") is the best element: big, worded, with a timer. [seen]
7. **Do the tips cover the arena?** Yes, a bit: the left-middle tip box overlapped telegraph starts and VFX in `ilse-r3-ginter` and the "Relic discovered" toast sits bottom-right. They are short and one-time, so tolerable, but they appear exactly at fight start when I most need clear view. [seen]
8. **Peak clutter.**
   - *King's Wrath (Gorrak I2):* the thick red ring band is very visible on grass (normal colour) and sits above the pods/pools; with ripening pools and the intermission banner on screen it is busy but readable. 3.2 s spacing not measured. [seen]
   - *Molten Tide:* clean and readable (see highlights). [seen]
   - *Floodfall:* clean; the cyan flood is distinct; red sector fills + gold safe quadrant on the shrinking island is the most information-dense frame I saw and it still reads. [seen]
   - *Act III horde at 2:00 (160 foes, Obelisk card open):* worst frame of the session. Hero findable only by its ring; red ground circles and pink projectile dots are readable but there are ~10 on screen. [seen] (`ilse-r3-a3peak.png`)
9. **Colour reliance (red-green).** Summarised in pain points 1–2. Safe on shapes: gold stripes, cones, rings, labels, cast strip. Not safe on colour alone: Timberfall lanes (fill), Gorrak slam disc, Vashkar's ring telegraph, sap pool rims, the red HP bar, pink projectiles vs grass, yellow beam vs yellow safe stripes. Blue/cyan things (hero ring, frost, banners) stay distinguishable. **A colour-blind mode (or at least a high-contrast telegraph toggle) is the single most valuable accessibility feature to add for this game.**
10. **Comfort.** Stills only: screen flashes are modest (white puffs and bursts, no full-screen flashes I caught), no strobing pattern visible; I cannot judge shake or hit-stop from stills. Text sizes in the HUD I read are acceptable except the boss frame's state chips and the STAGGER label (small, ~9–10 px at 1280 width). Death screen fine.
11. **Frame cost (`performance.now()` around `PT.play`).** Caveat: the driver freezes rAF and runs the sim headless-ish, so these are simulation costs per game-second, not render times. Act I horde ~24 ms per game-s (17 s in 411 ms); Act II horde (~39 foes) ~28 ms/s; Gorrak fight ~50 ms/s (30 s in 1.5 s with pods, pools, horde-free); Aurelith fight ~15–20 ms/s; Act III horde 50–70 foes ~11–25 ms/s (90–250 ms per 8–10 s); **peak Act III 161 foes ~48 ms/s** (143 ms for 3 s). Nothing alarming; I have no evidence of a frame-rate problem, but no true render-time measurement either. [data]

**Obelisk card readability (bonus):** the pre-fight card text ("Skips 130 s of horde", "≈ −2 levels after the Windfall", "Paid only if you win the boss fight") is explicit and well sized. The approach card, though, covers too much when it opens beside the hero (see pain point 5).

## Harness caveats
- Colour-blind findings come from a CSS/SVG filter I applied to the page (deuteranopia matrix) and then looked at through the pane screenshot (800x450). Real CVD varies; the *relative* result (hue-only distinctions collapse, brightness-only ones survive) is the point. The filter made the pane screenshot occasionally time out (the large canvas); I switched it off after each look. I never changed game state.
- All Act II/III findings are **[jumped]** and several runs used **assist** (0.25–0.5); the jump gives levels but no relics/HP scaling, so HP and DPS were not typical. Readability is unaffected, difficulty claims are not made.
- The bot is the hands: it stood in telegraphs and took contact hits that a human would avoid, which gave me more clips of damage events but is not evidence of unfairness.
- I could not judge animation, screen shake, hit-stop or audio from stills. Screenshots (`PT.shot`) have no HUD; I used pane screenshots for HUD checks.
- Not captured: Vashkar's Cinder Memory marks and ground-fire patches, Aurelith's second intermission, Aurelith's wyvern lightning rod (clean frame), the Rift Crystal Break Check bonus on screen.
