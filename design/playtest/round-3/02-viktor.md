# Viktor — hardcore buildcrafter · playtest round 3
**Verdict (in character).** The boss layer is a good stage and the obelisk cards are honest (the Act III "the run ends at the kill, so this chest becomes +20 shards" line is exactly what I want from a risk/reward card), but the *choice* is thin. Haste is a free lunch in Act III and a fair trade early, Echo and Wrath are almost impossible to take for a build-focused expert-hands run, and the build that cleans the fights is simply "two elements that make Steam, plus a damage-spike relic". Mono-element can Stagger now, but only slowly, and a mono pool runs out of on-element cards by level 8.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe (yes if Echo/Wrath became takeable and Steam stopped being the answer) · Domain score: 6/10

## Highlights (what worked, keep these)
- **Cards are legible and honest.** Cost / pay lines, "Paid only if you win the boss fight", the affix pre-shown on Wrath, and in Act III "Boss chest with 4 relics: becomes ≈ +20 shards (the run ends at the kill)". [read] (`PT.obelisks()` in every act)
- **Haste is a genuinely interesting trade at Act I.** At L4 I jumped to L8 (+4 levels) and "≈ -2 levels after the Windfall" was a fair label. The Windfall arrives as 4 stacked level-ups right at the Seal, so you must draft 4 cards in a boss arena; with the "level-ups wait until the boss has landed" fix that worked smoothly. [data] (run 2: L4 to L8 at 1:09)
- **The 3-phase raid structure with Winded/Downed windows is a good rhythm.** Intermission 8-9 s, Winded 3 s after, then a Break Check, felt like a real encounter; the death screen names the move and tells you what to do ("Club slam drops a red circle on you. Step out before it fills."). [read] [data]
- **Steam (Fire+Frost) and Superconduct have real, readable Stagger impact**: Break Checks broken on cue (e.g. "Prism Nova BROKEN" repeatedly), Downed windows of 4-8 s. [data] (run 3)
- **Vashkar's HUD chip "RESISTS FIRE 50%" plus intro line "Fire will not hurt him much."** is a good build-checking beat (it hit my mono-fire run's logic; Run 3 and 4 never cared). [read]
- **Wrath affix preview** (Reverberating / Rift Storm / Vengeful / Bulwark / Frenzied) is a good, concrete "what am I buying" line. [read]
- HP refills at each act start (115/130 to full, etc.) so attrition is act-scoped rather than run-scoped. [data] (runs 1, 3)

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **Echo and Wrath are not takeable in practice: channelling is a tax that grows fast.** In Act I at 0:54-1:06 (L4, thin horde) the Haste channel took ~12 s of play and cost 16 HP: fine. Once level-ups (every 4-8 s with Hoarder's / Magnetism / steam XP) and Elite Surges / Swarm come, the hands never complete the 4 s: I reached the ring (distance 2-4) six times and each time was out again within 1-2 s, or a level-up modal cancelled it. In run 5 walking to Wrath through a Swarm cost 90 HP in 20 s. [data] [seen] (shot: `playtest/out/viktor-r3-echo-ring.png`, `viktor-r3-echo-stuck.png`) · Severity: **major** (it makes Echo/Wrath "never-pick" for me even if the bot is partly to blame).
2. **Haste is a no-brainer in Act III.** The card shows "Windfall +2296 XP ≈ +85 shards" and the run *ends at the kill*, so the XP is worthless; I took it at L19, jumped to L23 and won with the fight at full HP. Cost: skipping 130 s of horde (no more relic bearers, but I had 5 relics). That is +75 shards (Haste Bounty) for free. The Act III chest is nerfed (+20 shards) but the Bounty is not. [data] [read] · Severity: **major** for the choice design (nothing else in Act III competes).
3. **Reaction/Steam still trivialises Stagger; mono-element cannot reach Break Checks.** Run 3 (Steam, 564 reactions): Gorrak War Horn broke 3/5, Prism Nova broke 4/4, Downed windows landed every ~15 s; stagger by source in one 25 s window: steam 90, superconduct 43, status 23, frozen 17. Run 1 (mono-fire, ~1.3 Stagger/s from Burning only): War Horn failed 5 of 5 (Gorrak's thresholds were 84, 109, 70, 137, 70 against a bar of 22-78). Run 4 (mono-storm) had almost no status Stagger (some windows 0) and the Gorrak fight ended in 55 s before the bar mattered. So the answer to "can a mono-element build Stagger now?" is: it fills the bar slowly, crystals and dodges are what lets you scrape a Down, and the Break Check is out of reach. [data] · Severity: **major**.
4. **Mono-element drafting runs dry.** Run 1 offered no fire card at L8 (Catalyst/Singularity/Blizzard) and I took off-element ones by L9-L13; three consecutive levels (L11, L13, L14) had zero fire cards. Same in run 4 (storm): L10 offered Catalyst, Spirit Orbs, Blizzard. There's one on-element skill upgrade per level at best, and Cinder Bolt was never offered a rank-up (r1 at L13) so the Starfall fusion path shown on Meteor ("Cinder Bolt r1/3 to Starfall") was unreachable. [data] [read] · Severity: **major** (fusions are unreachable for the starter skill).
5. **HP attrition mostly comes from intermission hazards and backhands, with few sustain choices.** Vashkar's Ashfall intermission cost me 50 HP (run 3), 23 (run 4); Gorrak's backhand hit the Pyromancer three times for 36, 38, 39 and the Arcanist for 35 (each 25-30 % of max HP), even though the lead is 0.85 s now. Sustain cards: Vitality (+0.4/s), Pyre Crown (0.5 HP per burning kill), Duelist's Mark (8 % per Perfect Dodge), Second Wind. The pickups that refilled me mid-fight (run 3: 101 to 164 across the Vashkar intermission and the adds; run 5: 76 to 177 over the Gorrak fight) are an excellent, unexplained mechanic. [data] · Severity: **minor/major**.
6. **Boss fight length varies hugely with build.** Fights: Gorrak 144 s (mono-fire), 92 s (Cryo steam), 55 s (mono-storm); Vashkar 142 s / 107 s; Aurelith 190 s (target 100-120 s). The same encounter is a long drag for a weak build and a sprint for a glass cannon; phase gates (66 %, 33 %) cancel a Break Check in progress (run 4) so a glass cannon never sees them. [data] · Severity: **minor**.

## Domain deep-dive (round-3 questions for Viktor)

**Are the trades legible, and is any obelisk a no-brainer or a never-pick?**
Cards are legible: cost lines, pay lines, "Paid only if you win", the affix named in advance, and honest Act III caveats ("the run ends at the kill"). [read]
- **Haste: the best-defined and best-paid choice.** Act I at L4: +4 levels now, -2 net, +33 shards, 4 relics, taken and worked. Act II: +1293 XP, +49 shards, -3 levels. Act III: +85 shards on the card and +75 paid, XP irrelevant. It is the *only* obelisk I could take, and in Act III it's a no-brainer. [data]
- **Echo and Wrath: functionally never-pick for me**, not because the card is bad (Echo: +10 % damage taken, boss at 55 % HP in Phase 2, 3 Boss Relics, +25 / +38 / +70 shards; Wrath: affix, Heat, 4 relics, 8-12-36 shards at Heat 1) but because I could not finish the channel, see pain #1. The Wrath Heat-1 payout of 8 shards (Act I) / 12 (Act II) is small next to Haste's 33 / 49 even before the extra risk. [read]
- Wrath's affixes I saw: Reverberating, Frenzied, Rift Storm, Vengeful (x3), Bulwark (x2). "Bulwark: Break Checks need 30 % more Stagger; Downed lasts 1 s less." is the only one a Stagger-build player will avoid. [read]

**Do the payouts feel worth the risk?** Haste: yes. Echo: I could not tell (never opened an Echo Chest). Wrath: "8 shards in total at Heat 1" feels light for "packs converge on you" + a harder boss. [read] [inferred]

**Can a mono-element build Stagger now? Does a reaction build still trivialise it?**
- Mono-fire (run 1): Burning gave ~1.3 Stagger/s; four Downed windows were from the bar slowly filling (4 s each), every Break Check failed (5/5). Fire is also resisted 50 % by Vashkar (HUD chip). [data]
- Mono-storm (run 4): near-zero Stagger from status; Gorrak died in 55 s anyway, so Stagger never mattered. [data]
- Reaction (run 2/3): Steam is the engine. Run 2 Arcanist filled War Horn's 60 in 4 s and broke it 3 of 3; run 3 Cryo (564 reactions) broke Prism Nova 4/4 and Aurelith went from 100 % to 33 % in about 2 minutes with zero damage taken in phase 3. Reaction fatigue is visible (Combust 34 then 15, Steam 32 then 9 across windows) but Steam from Fire+Frost remained a high-volume source. Verdict: **reaction builds still dominate Stagger; mono-element can raise a Down but not a Break Check.** [data]

**Do the 6 Boss Relics give real choices?**
I saw (as boss-chest/elite offers): Overclock, Ember Heart, Hoarder's Lantern, Conductor's Ring, Rime Shard, Duelist's Mark, Stormglass Boots, Pyre Crown, Blood Pact, Second Wind, Glass Sigil, Glacier Plate, Thorned Ward, Unstable Core, Prism Lens, Rift Magnet, Alchemist's Coil. These are the *general* relics, and I never saw a dedicated "Boss Relic" in an Echo/Wrath chest, so I can't judge the 6 specifically. Within what I saw: Hoarder's Lantern appeared as the filler in 5 offers and I took it twice (never for the build); Glass Sigil (+50 % damage, -40 % max HP) was a real "do I trust my hands" choice. Several offers were all off-build for a mono run (run 1 Act II: Conductor's Ring, Rime Shard, Hoarder's). Real choices exist for reaction builds, thin for mono. [read] (**not assessed: Echo Chest / Wrath Cache contents**)

**Does a sustain build change how boss fights play?**
Yes, modestly. Run 3 (Second Wind + Glacier Plate + Vitality + Duelist's Mark): Gorrak cost me only ~50 HP total, and at Vashkar I went from 101 to 164 HP in a single intermission; Act III phase 3 cost 0 HP. Run 5 (two Vitality, 190 HP): HP rose from 76 to 177 across the Gorrak fight. The mid-fight healing source appears to be health drops from adds, regen and relics. It makes the fights *survivable* more than it changes tactics: you still dodge, you just have a bigger buffer, and Glacier Plate (-3 % per frozen foe) needs frozen adds that don't exist in some phases. A glass cannon (run 4, 122 HP, Glass-Sigil-style damage) trades that buffer for speed (Gorrak in 55 s) but then dies in the Act III horde (122 HP vs Storm Wyvern + Naga: 115 to 0 in 8 s). [data]

**Build variety / what would keep me playing 20 hours?**
Archetypes I saw: Steam (Fire+Frost) reactions, Superconduct (Storm+Frost), mono-element, fusion (Hailstorm), sustain. The reaction family is clearly the strongest. Fusion needs both skills at rank 3 and starters can't rank up in the draft (pain #4). To keep me playing: a way to commit to a single element deliberately (element lock), the Echo/Wrath payouts (and "Heat 2-3" caches) reachable, and tier scaling I didn't get to test.

## Harness caveats
- `PT.channel` is walk-and-stand with hands that also dodge and fight: it likely fails more than a human would. I'd put 50 % of pain #1 on the harness (level-up modals also cancel the channel) and 50 % on the design (Swarm, Elite Surge, XP-rich builds levelling every 4-8 s). So **Echo and Wrath payouts, the Wrath Cache, Echo Chest and their effect on the Stagger bar / fight are unobserved.**
- Bot hands dodge backhands badly (35-39 each time for Pyro/Arcanist), so the damage figures are an upper bound; expert hands also can't use pillars, which Aurelith run 3 may have benefitted from by luck.
- Tutorial overlays and the run time skew: the first run's act clock lagged 12 s behind game time (primer).
- Shot crops were not used heavily (3D canvas only, no HUD), so "readable" claims rely on `PT.ui()` text.
