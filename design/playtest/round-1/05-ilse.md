# Ilse — comfort & readability · playtest round 1
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).** The art is lovely and the enemy projectiles/red telegraphs are mostly easy to pick out against grass, but at peak density my own spell effects (steam clouds, fireballs, thundercloud, glyphs) swallow the hero, and the right-hand reaction feed turns into a wall of text. I lost my hero for more than a second in most of my worst-case frames, and the geyser telegraphs on the Barrens' tan ground are nearly invisible. Shake and hit-stop were gentle; a couple of full-frame bright washes and a lot of slow-mo are my only comfort worries.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe (yes if I could turn VFX down) · Domain score: 5.5/10

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **Enemy projectiles are the easiest thing on screen.** Magenta/pink orbs with a bright white core read instantly on green forest, tan barrens and teal water, and they are shaped distinctly from every spell. [seen] (shots: `dev/shots/pt-ilse-a3-peak.png`, `pt-ilse-a3-arc-hitstop.png`, `pt-ilse-a1-swarm.png`)
- **Red ground telegraphs are bold and consistent in Act III**: double concentric rings and filled cone wedges are readable by shape and brightness, not just hue. [seen] (shot: `pt-ilse-a3-storm-lowhp.png`, `pt-ilse-a3-boss-c.png`)
- **The hero's under-foot ring** (orange for Pyromancer, cyan for Cryomancer/Stormcaller) is the one thing that reliably locates the hero when I do find it, plus the dithered see-through foliage when the hero is behind a tree. [seen] (shot: `pt-ilse-a2-boss-p1.png`)
- **Prism Ward bubbles** are clear, large and distinct from the enemy inside them; the "mind the lightning circles" tip is a good, short, plain sentence. [seen][read]
- **Screen shake is genuinely low.** Over ~65 s of sampled 0.1 s steps in Act III (boss P2 and a 100+ foe horde) mean trauma was 0.01–0.03, peak 0.39, and only 1.3 % of steps over 0.3 in the worst sample. Hit-stop max 0.09 s. [data]
- **Performance is comfortable in the sim**: 10 s of game time cost ~390 ms wall at Act III peak (~100 foes) versus ~100–240 ms in Act I; 5 s at 130 foes cost ~230 ms. [data]
- **Low-HP state is unmistakable** (strong red vignette + bar), which is a good feedback channel. [seen] (HUD screenshot at 12/90).

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **The hero disappears inside my own spell effects at peak density.** In `pt-ilse-a3-peak.png` the Cryomancer is inside a white steam-burst cloud, orange blast and shard burst; in `pt-ilse-a3-arc-flash.png` and `pt-ilse-a3-arc-hitstop.png` (Arcanist) I could not find the hero in under a second at all; in `pt-ilse-a3-boss-c.png` a dark Thunderhead cloud sits directly on top of the hero; in `pt-ilse-a1-swarm.png` a fireball + chain-lightning flash covers an orange-coloured Pyromancer on an orange-brown dirt road. The only reliable locator is the small under-foot ring, and that too is washed out by the smoke. This is pillar 1 ("your character… always readable") failing at exactly the moments the pillar is about. [seen] · Severity: major
2. **Reaction feed + chain counter flood the right edge.** At Act III the feed shows ~14 stacked chips (OVERLOAD ×2 / STEAM BURST ×6 / SUPERCONDUCT ×79 / …, each ~26 px high) down half of the 720 px height, over the play area, with ~37 chip nodes alive in the DOM; the chain counter (49.6 px) reads "×1648" then "×2993". Many chips duplicate the same reaction with tiny counts ("overload ×2", "overload ×3", "combust ×1"). The numbers (×1933 steam bursts) are jargon/noise that no player can parse mid-fight. Also covers the lightning-strike beam and enemies at the right edge. [seen][read] (HUD capture at 800×600 pane, `PT.ui()` text) · Severity: major
3. **Act II geyser telegraph is almost invisible.** The Fissure Geysers' warning is thin cream/amber cracks on tan-ochre ground (`pt-ilse-a2-geyser-tele.png`, `pt-ilse-a2-lowhp.png`). It breaks the "red-orange = danger" rule, has no ring or fill, and has the lowest contrast of any hazard I saw. I took "map hazard: lava" damage (11) at 28 HP without ever noticing what hit me. A colour-blind player or anyone in a busy fight would never see it. [seen][data] · Severity: major
4. **Colour is carrying too much on the Act III screen (CVD risk).** (a) Three different "reds" are in play at once: red dragonkin/kobolds, red telegraph rings/cones and pink/magenta shots; on green grass a protan/deutan player gets dark, similar-looking blobs (`pt-ilse-a3-storm-lowhp.png`). (b) Prism Ward elements are shown only as bubble colour (orange, blue, yellow, purple); I could not see an icon on the bubbles, so "which element is this ward?" is colour-only. (c) Gorrak's Phase 2 toxin ring is thin lime-green on lime-green grass (`pt-ilse-a1-boss-p2.png`), and the lightning-strike circle in Act III is pale white-blue on teal water, both off the red-orange palette the pillar promises. (d) XP gems are blue and gold diamonds, only differentiated by hue, and tiny in clutter. [seen][inferred] · Severity: major
5. **Slow-mo and bright full-frame washes.** In the sampled Act III windows the time-scale was below 1.0 for ~10 % of steps (21/199 and 15/149 steps) and `_skyFlash` reached 0.5 with ~1–2 % of steps over 0.3; the hit-stop frame in `pt-ilse-a3-arc-hitstop.png` is a whole-screen pale-green/white wash (bloom or aberration kick) that lowers contrast of everything including the hero. Not dangerous but a comfort/readability dent, and there is no "reduce flashes" option, only the Shake setting. [data][seen] · Severity: minor
6. **The boss was off-screen for ~45 s in Act III with no camera help.** Aurelith sat ~36–38 u away at 99 % while I fought the horde; the one orange beam/pillar at the top of the frame was the only hint. The boss at P2 (after I teleported next to it) is a semi-transparent wireframe-ish silhouette at the top edge of the frame, hard to identify (`pt-ilse-a3-boss-p2.png`). [seen][data] · Severity: minor
7. **Hero is small and dark against dark ground in Act III.** The Stormcaller (dark hat) reads as a smudge next to a pond (`pt-ilse-a3-storm-lowhp.png`); only the cyan ring gives it away. [seen] · Severity: minor
8. **Small UI text.** At 1280×720 the smallest HUD text is 11.2 px (key caps, rank pips, "auto", "lv") in dark ink on gold; reaction chips are 14.4 px; tooltips 12 px. Fine on a desktop monitor, borderline on a laptop/Steam Deck. [read] · Severity: polish

## Bugs & oddities
- With `tutorial:false`, `PT.ui()` still contains "skip tutorial" and "tip — A Shrine has appeared…" text (probably hidden DOM, but it shows in the text dump; and the tips are tutorial-style at 2:33 of Act I). Repro: start any run with `tutorial:false`, call `PT.ui()`.
- End-screen text read at the moment of death: "+0 rift shards total 0" although `PT.end()` returned `shardsEarned: 103, totalShards: 103` (likely a count-up animation; harness may read it too early).
- `unlockAll` meta lists Cryomancer as unlocked at init, yet the first `PT.end()` reported `newlyUnlocked: ["cryomancer"]` and an end-screen "new riftbound Cryomancer" banner (harness artefact probably).
- `PT.state().screen` says `playing` while a level-up decision is pending after `PT.jump`; `PT.play` then returns `{error: 'a decision is waiting'}`. Check `PT.decision()` first (cost me two calls).
- Aurelith held at 99 % for 46 s of act time with the bot never reaching it (boss ~38 u away, no damage from the boss during that time). Bot-hands issue or the boss lacks pressure; not my domain.
- Reaction feed / counter show absurd tallies ("steam burst ×1933", chain ×2993 at level 30 on a jumped run), which suggests the chain/reaction count is a running total and not a streak.

## Domain deep-dive
**Where did I lose track of my hero?** [seen] Act III peak with a Cryomancer in a steam-burst cloud (`a3-peak.png`); Arcanist in a Meteor/Singularity/glyph pile (`a3-arc-flash.png`, `a3-arc-hitstop.png`); Stormcaller at the pond with a dark hat and low health (`a3-storm-lowhp.png`); Pyromancer on the orange-brown dirt road under Fireball + Chain Lightning flash (`a1-swarm.png`); Pyromancer behind trees and Singularity pile in Act II (`a2-lowhp.png`, `a2-geyser-tele.png`). The hero was findable in under a second in: `a3-peak-b.png`, `a3-boss-p2.png`, `a1-boss-p2b.png`, `a1-boss-p2.png` (ring clear, few effects overlapping). Act I swarm moment itself was fine, a good fraction of the 2:15 swarm is not yet on screen at the spawn ring.

**Can I see every red telegraph and every enemy projectile?** Enemy projectiles: yes in every shot, including Act II and Act III peaks. Red telegraphs (Act III circles, cones; Vashkar's red ring; Gorrak's red circle): yes, with the caveat that they are partly hidden behind bright explosion VFX (cones in `a3-peak.png` run underneath the white steam). Exceptions: the Act II geyser cracks and Gorrak's lime toxin ring, the Act III lightning circles on water (all off-palette, low contrast).

**Pickups distinguishable?** XP gems: blue diamonds (Act I–II) and gold diamonds (Act III), both tiny; they are clearly visible on green and tan but drown in explosion clutter, and the two colours are distinguished by hue only. The shrine (gold ring + beam) and crystals (cyan/purple dashed rings) are large and distinct. I did not see a health pickup or the Relic Bearer's marker clearly in my shots, so I cannot judge them. [seen][inferred]

**Colour-blind (red-green) loss of information?** Likely losses: (1) telegraph red vs green grass is saved by the bold ring/wedge shapes and bright rim, but red dragonkin enemies share that hue (threat vs target ambiguity); (2) Prism Ward elements rely on bubble colour only; (3) green Gorrak toxin ring and pale lightning ring depend on hue/lightness only; (4) element colours (orange fire, cyan frost, yellow storm, purple arcane) mostly differ in lightness so fire/storm are close; (5) the low-HP vignette is red on green but the bar number and bar length also carry it. [inferred] No colour-blind option exists on the title screen (only Shake Full/Reduced/Off per DESIGN.md §11).

**Text in `PT.ui()` / DOM:** HUD labels are short and plain ("Aurelith in 0:06", "Fusion forged — Storm Halo, Chain Lightning + Spirit Orbs", "Relic discovered — Hoarder's Lantern: +30% XP. Chests offer 4 relics."). Level-up cards: "Might → +12% damage. passive · always on · max rank 5" and "rank up Thunderhead → Strikes every 0.6 s." — clear, but rank-up cards show only the new value, not the old one. Reaction feed duplicates and the ×1000+ numbers are the legibility problem (see pain 2). Smallest text: 11.2 px (keycaps/pips), reaction chips 14.4 px, chain 49.6 px, tooltips 12 px at 1280×720. Dense but not overlong; no truncated strings seen. [read]

**Screen shake / flashes / comfort:** (sampled via `__game.engine.trauma`, `_selfTrauma`, `_skyFlash`, `__game._hitStop`, 0.1 s steps) Act III boss P2 horde: mean trauma 0.012, max 0.19, 0 % > 0.3, 6/199 hit-stop steps (max 0.09 s), 21/199 slow-mo steps, `_skyFlash` max 0.5 (1 % over 0.3). Stormcaller low-HP: mean 0.026, max 0.25. Arcanist at 100 foes: mean 0.028, max 0.39, 1.3 % over 0.3, 4/149 hit-stop, 15/149 slow-mo. Verdict: shake is well tamed; slow-mo is frequent (~10 % of steps) and the bright wash on reaction frames is the only stronger flash risk. [data]

**Performance:** Sim: Act I calm (~30–50 foes) 18 s of game time in ~1.2 s including drafts, 12 s in ~240 ms; Act I swarm/peak 82 foes; Act III with 100–130 foes: 10 s in ~390 ms, 5 s in ~230 ms (so roughly 2–3× the calm cost; no spikes). Render (hidden pane, `gl.finish` after 10 `engine.render()` calls, indicative only): Act I swarm 1.3 ms with 133 draw calls and 0.87 M triangles; Act II 1.0 ms with 99 calls / 0.7 M tris; Act III peak 2.3–2.8 ms with 165–263 calls / 1.03–1.08 M tris. Triangle count is high (~1 M) but the measured cost is small; real frame time on a laptop GPU will be higher, and these numbers were taken in a hidden tab with no vsync. [data]

## Harness caveats
- Browser pane is hidden/virtual: render ms are indicative only. The pane is 1280×720 (a `computer` screenshot downscales to 800×600), so on-screen text sizes in the HUD capture look smaller than they are. A couple of later `computer` screenshots timed out.
- `PT.shot` has no HUD, so I used a few `computer` screenshots for HUD/low-HP/feed views.
- The bot's hands decide where the hero stands; a human would reposition to see more clearly. Boss P2 views were [forced] by setting `__game.boss.hp` and (once) teleporting the player beside the boss; Act II/III views were [jumped] and mostly [assisted].
- Drafting used a "new skill first, else rank, else first card" policy (I was the brain for readability, not for build quality), and relics were picked blindly (first). Builds are therefore not representative.
- Deaths (Pyromancer vs Vashkar, Stormcaller at 0:52 in Act III) are not evidence of difficulty here.
- I did not test the title-screen Shake setting, the Sanctum or the level-up modal visually.
