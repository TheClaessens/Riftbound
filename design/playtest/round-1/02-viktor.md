# Viktor — the hardcore buildcrafter · playtest round 1
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).** There is a real build game in here — exclusive fusion forks, a few relic engines that genuinely rewrite a run, rerolls that work as a tool — but it collapses toward one archetype (storm + frost reaction spam under Prism Lens / Catalyst / Alchemist's Coil) by Act III, the pool is clogged with dead cards, and the meta is fully exhausted after ~5 runs (3,498 shards banked with nothing to buy). Tiers 1→4 felt like "same game, bigger HP bars" rather than new challenges. I'd come back for the fusion hunt, but not for 20 hours as shipped.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe · Domain score: 6/10

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **Exclusive fusion forks are the best decision in the game.** At Static Field r3 + Spark r3 + Blizzard r1 I had to pick Tempest *or* keep Static Field for Thunderhead; fusion consumes both parents and frees two slots. Run 6 offered Cataclysm vs Starfall on one card screen. This is the "I'm building something" feeling. [data: runs 4, 6]
- **Card hints ("Fusion path: + Fireball → Plasma Orb") make planning possible** and the guaranteed fusion card at rank 3/3 is a clean, legible payoff. A hardcore player can route a whole run from L2. [read]
- **Rerolls are a real tool.** In run 4 I needed Static Field r3: a single reroll at L8 produced it and Tempest arrived at L9 (2:27). Fortune r3 / Exile r3 (5 rerolls / 4 banishes) are the attunements a build-crafter feels. [data]
- **Relics with identity create real run-to-run variety**: Pyre Crown turned Pyromancer into a sustain build (13 → 130 HP in ~8 s), Glass Sigil + Second Wind as a risk/insurance pair, Ember Heart making Combust scale, Prism Lens as an engine, Rime Shard for freeze builds. [data, run 1/6/7]
- **Difficulty reads fairly by tier for the tank**: Cryomancer took ~0 damage at T1, ~0 until Act III at T3 with 170–250 HP; the glass Pyromancer died to the same Boars at T0 and T2. Class identity (tank vs glass) is clear. [data]
- **Act III is a visual spectacle that stays readable at 50+ Chain**: elite telegraphs (red discs/rectangles), the blue/yellow crystals, boss wind-ups all legible amid reaction noise. [seen] (shot: `dev/shots/pt-viktor-run3-act3.png`, `pt-viktor-run7-aurelith.png`, `pt-viktor-run8-aurelith.png`)
- **Boss TTK by class tells a story**: Pyromancer deletes Gorrak (17–19 s), Cryomancer at 79–94 s, Stormcaller 90 s — Gorrak is a real build check even at T0–T2. [data]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **The shard formula's chain term is runaway, and chain itself is broken by Prism Lens.** Run 3: maxChain 3,059, run 7: 7,047, run 8: 2,246 (HUD showed `CHAIN x989`, `x1564`). `floor(maxChain/10)` therefore added 305 / 704 / 224 shards, i.e. a third of the payout, versus 71–296 for the deaths. A win at T1 paid 859 (6.5× my best loss). Echo-spam (Superconduct echoing Superconduct) is the entire economy. Chain's XP bonus caps at +25 % but the shard term has no cap. [data] · Severity: **major**
2. **The meta is exhausted after ~5 runs and then has no sink.** Total attunement cost is only 1,440; I earned 4,938 over 9 runs and finished with 3,498 shards idle. After run 7 nothing I earn means anything, and there are only 4 classes / 5 tiers as unlock targets. This directly answers "what would keep me playing 20 hours": nothing yet. [data] · Severity: **major**
3. **One archetype dominates: storm/frost reaction spam with Prism Lens + Catalyst + Alchemist's Coil + Rift Magnet.** Both of my wins and my best assisted run were this; chain went to x1,500–x7,000. Fire/burn builds (Pyromancer) kill Gorrak fastest but fall to Act II melee; Arcanist's identity (Resonance) never turns on. The passives converge too: every deep run ended on Might 5 / Catalyst 5 / Haste 5 / Vitality 5 / Volley 2 (5 distinct slots are always the same ones). [data] · Severity: **major**
4. **Arcanist is a trap class.** It is unlocked by killing Gorrak (first thing a new player earns!) and then it is the weakest class: run 2 died to Gorrak (28 % left), run 5 died at Vashkar. With only Arcane Bolt and no second skill offered, there were **0 reactions in run 2** (Resonance +dmg/+CDR per Chain never starts). Spirit Orbs (the whole Arcane Barrage plan) was first offered at L15 in run 5 and never in runs 2's first 8 levels. [data] · Severity: **major**
5. **Card pool dilution and dead cards.** Magnetism, Swiftness, Amplify, Precision and rank cards like "+20 % radius" / "+15 % radius" show up in nearly every hand (I counted ~7 "all-weak" hands; at L8–L11 hands such as `Precision / Singularity / Swiftness` repeat). Once 5 passives are owned the pool offers non-owned active skills I can't use (4 slots) in place of rank-ups; rank-ups of fusion parents are sparse (needed 2–3 rerolls for Static Field r3, Blizzard r3). Meteor, the key card of two Pyromancer fusions, was first offered at L11 in run 1 and at L11 in run 6 (never earlier despite 3 rerolls). Banish redraws only that one card, so it doesn't narrow the pool. [data] · Severity: **major**
6. **Act II melee (War Boar) is the real wall, and it kills on a timer, not a pattern.** 4 of 5 Act II deaths: Boar damage windows of 83, 164, 208, 105, 158 HP; HP bars of 130–160 melt in a few seconds. Pyre Crown healed me to full in ~8 s and the next Boar pack still killed me. Nothing in the card pool counters a charge (no knockback relic besides Unstable Core/Overload, no slows besides Frost). [data] · Severity: **minor/major for squishy builds**
7. **Tiers are bigger numbers, not new challenges.** Gorrak TTK with a similar build: 17 s (T2) → 140 s (T4); Cryo took 0 damage across T1 and T3 Acts I–II. In 4 tiers I saw no new enemy type, modifier or mechanic (Act III: Whelps, Crag Drakes, Wyverns at T1 and T3 alike). T4–T5 are pure HP/damage multipliers. [data/inferred] · Severity: **major** for retention
8. **Aurelith (Act III boss) drags for 6–10 minutes.** Kill times 344 s (T1) and 593 s (T3), against a target of 60–90 s. The bar sits at 91 % for ~90 s and at 52 % for 2 min (phase transition?) while my build deals thousands of reaction damage. For a tank it's boring; for the assisted Stormcaller it's an attrition fight that ended in death at 26 %. [data] · Severity: **major**

Arcanist reached L5 at 1:57 and L6 at the boss. Mid-Act I is "no new cards for a minute". Conversely, from L30 on levels come every second with nothing left to buy (passives capped, fusions at r3): L66 at the end of run 7. [data] · Severity: **minor**

Some hands are all-bad for the class (Pyromancer: Conductor's Ring / Glacier Plate / Duelist's Mark). Glass Sigil appeared in ~8 of ~35 offers. [data] · Severity: **minor**

## Bugs & oddities
- `PT.banish(i)` replaced only the banished card (the other two stayed); it does not reshuffle the hand. [data] (cosmetic, but it makes banish weaker than it reads).
- Fusion at rank 3 removes **both** parent skills from the loadout (run 3: skills went `Ice Shard, Static, Blizzard` → `Static, Hailstorm`). Intended per design but it is easy to miss: the starter slot vanishes. In run 7 the auto-draft then re-offered and fused Blizzard + Ice Shard again into Hailstorm *after* I had already built Absolute Zero (three fusions at the end — feels more like a bug than a design, there is no cap).
- Stat screens: after the Gorrak kill the state sits in `screen: "shift"` with 2–3 level-ups queued that resolve only after `PT.play`. (run 5, 3:44). [data]
- Chain counter reached ×3,059 / ×7,047; HUD formatted it as `×989 ×50!` — probably meant as a chain-milestone ping, unreadable at that scale.
- Pyromancer fresh HP is 90 with a −10 HP tilt; with Glass Sigil −40 % it becomes 54 HP, which is simply dead against a Boar. The risk relics do not show the number of HP you end up with in the card text.
- I accidentally let the auto-draft take Conductor's Ring on the Arcanist (no shock source) — my tooling slip, not a game bug, but it shows how "shock" relics are dead for non-storm classes.

## Domain deep-dive
**How many genuinely different builds exist?** By fusions: 10 recipes, 6 of which I forged (Hailstorm, Plasma Orb, Absolute Zero, Storm Halo, Tempest, Cataclysm; not seen: Starfall, Frostfire Comet, Thunderhead, Arcane Barrage). Three are class-gated starter fusions (Starfall/Hailstorm/Tempest) and Arcane Barrage is Arcanist-only, so a given class has ~3–4 plausible fusion routes — Pyromancer: Starfall / Cataclysm / Comet / Plasma Orb; Cryomancer: Hailstorm / Absolute Zero / Thunderhead / Comet; Stormcaller: Tempest / Thunderhead / Plasma Orb / Storm Halo; Arcanist: Barrage / Absolute Zero / Storm Halo. So there are 12–16 theoretical build paths, but only ~3 distinct play styles in practice: **burn/starter-DPS (Pyro)**, **control/AoE freeze (Cryo, Arcanist AZ)**, **storm/frost reaction engine (Stormcaller/Cryo)**. By the end of every long run the passives are identical and the winner is whichever one carried Prism Lens, so the *effective* count of viable archetypes is ~2–3 per class. Genuinely different runs came from class + first fusion + one engine relic, not from the card-by-card choices.

**Which choices are traps or no-brainers?**
- No-brainers: Volley r1/r2 on any projectile starter, Might, starter-skill ranks (Cinder Bolt r2–r5, Arcane Bolt r2–r5, Ice Shard r3), Prism Lens, Alchemist's Coil/Catalyst in a reaction build, Second Wind at T2+, Static Field r3+. Starter ranks sometimes feel "forced" because skipping them is a visible DPS loss.
- Traps / dead: Magnetism, Swiftness, Amplify, Precision (almost always the weakest card), "+20 % radius" ranks, Conductor's Ring outside storm, Overclock (never useful in 6 sightings), Hoarder's Lantern (the 4-choice benefit only pays with many chests), Glass Sigil on a ≤ 100 HP class without Second Wind, Duelist's Mark with a bot that rarely perfect-dodges; Blood Pact on low-HP classes (drain).
- Real forks: Tempest vs Thunderhead; Cataclysm vs Starfall; Pyre Crown vs Lantern; Glass Sigil vs Second Wind; Absolute Zero vs Hailstorm from the same Frost Nova hub.

**Does Tier N feel like a new challenge or just bigger numbers?** Bigger numbers. Compare Gorrak at T2 (17 s Pyro, 43 s Arcanist) with T4 (140 s) and see Cryo with zero damage at both T1 and T3. I observed no tier-specific enemies, affixes, boss attacks or map changes between T0–T4; the only differences are HP/damage and shard multiplier `1 + 0.25·tier`. The tier shard reward (×1.25–×2) is far smaller than the chain-term swing, so tiers are not even financially compelling. Act II is the wall at T2+, Act III only gets reached by tank builds.

**Reward pacing (shards, unlock pace, attunements).** Fresh save: Run 1 (132 shards): bought Fortune ×2, Exile, Vigor, Might. Run 2 (71): Might ×2, Insight, Swiftcast — the first two runs bought ~6 ranks. After the T1 win (859) I bought 12 ranks in one sitting; after run 7 I was capped. Unlock pace: Arcanist unlocked on run 1 (Gorrak kill at 3:28); Cryomancer needs 4 reactions (I had 3/6 after 2 fresh runs: Combust, Steam, Overload — I'd have unlocked it on run 3); Stormcaller needs Act III, which only the Cryomancer could reach — a nice dependency, but it means a fresh-save player's third class sits behind a class that requires reaching 4 reactions. The early curve is good; the late curve is a dead end.

**What would keep me playing 20 hours?** (1) A real shard sink (see rec 2). (2) Tier modifiers/affixes and boss variants so T3–T5 are different. (3) Relic/fusion unlocks that expand the pool rather than just exhaust it (codex 16/18 relics, 6/10 fusions seen in 9 runs). (4) Better draft agency: rank-up pity, banish that narrows the pool. (5) A per-class "challenge" or ascension that restricts passives to push off-meta builds. As it stands I'd have "solved" the game (storm/frost + Prism Lens) within ~6 runs.

## Harness caveats
- The bot's hands are why the Pyromancer/Stormcaller/Arcanist died in Act I–II; I can't separate "weak class" from "bot kiting weakness" (Arcanist's blink playstyle likely needs a human). I judged Arcanist's **lack of reactions** and **draft access**, which are independent of the hands.
- Runs 5–9 used an auto-draft for the filler picks (priority list), which sometimes takes odd cards (e.g. Conductor's Ring on Arcanist, Blizzard re-fusion in run 7, skipping the Tempest rank). Treat run 8–9 builds as sloppy; they are only used for Act III / Tier 4 content questions.
- `assist: 0.5` on runs 8 and 9 [assisted]; Act III damage numbers there are halved. Run 7 (T3 win) was unassisted but the Cryomancer is the tank the brief calls out.
- Tier 4 evidence is a single assisted run with 2 skills and bad rolls; tier 5 untested.
- XP/level timings are real-run times (the harness runs faster than real time but game-time is real); the 17–21 min total run length (vs the ~10.5 min design) is therefore a real finding, driven by Act III's boss.
- Screenshots have no HUD; the boss crop sometimes missed the boss (run 8).
