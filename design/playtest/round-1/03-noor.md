# Noor — genre newcomer · playtest round 1
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).**  I came in knowing nothing and the game was kinder than I expected: the little "Rift Primer" panel and the "Reacts with your fire: Combust" lines on the cards taught me the core idea (mix two elements = bang) faster than I thought possible. But I never learned *why* I died, "fusion" turned out to work differently from what the screens told me, and a lot of words (Chill, Echo, Adrenaline, "u", Chain's reward) are never explained. I'd happily try again, mostly to see more fusions, but I wouldn't know how to get better at it.
Fun: 6.5/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe · Domain score: 6/10

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **The Primer panel is short, gold-pulsing and in plain English.** Five steps, each one sentence ("Hold lmb to cast Cinder Bolt. Move with wasd." -> "Press space to dash... PERFECT dodge" -> "A Frost Rift Crystal! Shoot it while foes are inside its ring" -> "Now hit the Chilled foes with your Fire! ... REACTION"). After it I could say "two elements on one enemy = explosion". [read] (shot: `dev/shots/pt-noor-first-seconds.png`)
- **"Reacts with your fire: COMBUST" hint on every new-skill card** is the single best teaching device. It told me *which card to take to make reactions happen* without any manual. [read]
- **First-time "NEW REACTION" toast spells the recipe** ("Steam Burst - Fire + Frost: scalding AoE explosion", "Overload - Fire + Storm...", "Superconduct - Frost + Storm"). I learned 5 of 6 reactions from toasts, not from the primer. [read] [seen]
- **Fusion card is unmistakable** when it finally appears: rainbow header, both parent icons on the sides, "Fuses Ice Shard + Blizzard", and a clear "Unlocks FUSION: Hailstorm!" line on the rank-up that sets it up. [seen] (shot: Hailstorm card in run 2; `dev/shots/pt-noor-hailstorm.png` for the result)
- **Relic cards have a useful footer**: "5 reactions set off this run" under reaction relics, "Flame Dash - 1 charge" under dodge relics (although I did not understand the latter). [seen]
- **Act tips** at the right moments ("The orcs of the Barrens resist Fire (50%)", "Some foes carry a Prism Ward - immune to one element. Any reaction on a warded foe shatters its ward", "Every attack is telegraphed in red") are clear. [read]
- **Death screen "Ledger"** is tidy: class, act, time, level, kills, max chain, reaction counts with bars, relics. [seen]
- **Level-up modal layout** (number key hint, NEW / PASSIVE / RANK UP tag, element + "active - rmb" footer, cooldown "1.4 -> 1.3s") is very easy to read. [seen]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **"Why did I die?" cannot be answered from the screen.** The death screen says "The Rift claims you" and shows my stats, but never who or what killed me. The harness's breakdown calls most damage "enemy projectile" (48 of 57 in run 1, 53 of 53 in run 3) - I would not know whether that is an archer, a mage or a boss. In the close-up at 36 HP my character is buried under a steam/overload cloud and I cannot see where I am or what is aiming at me. [data] [seen] (shot: `dev/shots/pt-noor-lowhp.png`) · Severity: major
2. **Fusions are explained wrongly/incompletely.** (a) The death screen says "Raise two skills to rank 3 and the Rift offers a fusion." In run 2 I had Blizzard r3 and Chain Lightning r3 and nothing was offered - it only works for specific pairs. (b) Cards say "Fusion path -> Starfall" on a rank-up *without naming the partner*, and I put Cinder Bolt to r3 without ever being offered Meteor again. (c) The fusion card does not appear the moment the second parent hits r3; it comes at the *next* level-up, so for one level-up I thought it had failed. (d) Nothing warned me the fusion **consumes both parent skills** (my bar went from 5 skills to 3 and two slots were suddenly empty). (e) The card pool does not seem to prioritise the missing parent: Ice Shard r2/r3 did not show up for ~6 level-ups. [read] [data] · Severity: major
3. **The primer teaches four buttons and one idea, then stops.** It never mentions RMB/Q/E/R skills, aiming skills at the cursor, how the level-up cards work, Reroll/Banish, relic chests, shrines or the bottom skill bar. I only saw "active - rmb" on a card footer and had no way to know whether a skill fires by itself. Also it ends after ~16 s of game time with "Survive three minutes, then slay the Troll King" - good goal, but it does not say what the bar at the top is. [read] · Severity: major
4. **Vocabulary wall.** Words I met and never found explained: "u" (as in "within 10 u"), "Chill / Frozen / Shock stacks" ("Foes Freeze at 3 Chill instead of 5"), "echo" (Prism Lens), "Adrenaline", "Flame Dash - 1 charge", "Relic Bearer", "Elite Surge", "Catalyst", "Amplify (area of effect)", "Volley", "statusPower"-style jargon on cards, "auto" vs "active", "rank / max rank 5", "Banish" and "Reroll" (no tooltip). I had to guess every one. [read] · Severity: major
5. **Chain is described three different ways.** Primer: "Reactions in quick succession build CHAIN for bonus XP." HUD: a panel showing "x86 ... +50% dmg" and a "x50!" milestone. Numbers climb to x647, which means nothing to me. I also never learned the time window: in run 2 (all frost, no reactions at first) Chain stayed at 0 for the first 90 s with no hint why. I cannot tell whether I should care about Chain. [read] [seen] · Severity: major
6. **First reaction toast is confusing.** At 0:07, before the primer's crystal step, the toast "COMBUST - Arcane on a Burning foe" popped up although I only owned a fire bolt (a purple crystal exploded nearby). I thought I was missing something. [read] · Severity: minor
7. **Chaotic screen at high Chain.** HUD is cramped at 800x600 (the "CHAIN" caption sits under the big number, reaction ticker, +dmg bar and buffs all stack on the right); the player avatar is small and often invisible under VFX. [seen] · Severity: minor/major for a newcomer
8. **Level-up spam late in a run.** In run 2 I got 3-4 level-ups within 1-3 game seconds (e.g. 7:22, 7:22, 7:23; 9:38, 9:39) each with its own modal. By the end I was auto-clicking without reading - exactly the opposite of "choose carefully". [data] · Severity: minor
9. **Class selection feels arbitrary for a first-timer.** Pyromancer (the only choice) has the lowest HP (90) and Act II orcs resist fire; the Cryomancer I unlocked after dying once took zero damage all game. Nothing told me which class is gentle. The wide difficulty gap hides what I did wrong in runs 1 and 3. [data] · Severity: minor
10. **Meta feedback is unclear.** The death screen shows "+0 rift shards / total 5" even though 173 shards were awarded (harness may skip the count-up). Abandoning a run pays nothing and, in run 2, reaching Act III while abandoning did not unlock Stormcaller ("Reach the Stormwater Reach (Act III)"). [seen] [data] · Severity: minor

## Domain deep-dive
**What did I understand after the primer?** Move with WASD, hold left mouse to shoot, Space dashes and makes me untouchable for a moment (and "just as an attack lands = PERFECT dodge"), crystals are shootable bombs that put a status on everything near them, and "two elements on one foe" = a reaction (Steam Burst = Fire + Frost). I understood the *idea* but not the *hotkeys* beyond those, nor what to do with the bar at the bottom. [read]

**Which words/icons confused me?** See pain point 4. Icons: the small diamond pips under skill slots (what are they?), the "2 / 3 / 4" numbers on slots (rank? cooldown?), the "auto" pill, the two relic-style circles at the right of the bar, and the elite/relic-bearer symbol. The element icons on level-up cards (hexagon, diamond) are pretty but I could not tell elements apart by shape alone without the footer text ("frost - active - q"). [seen]

**Could I explain Chain, reactions and fusions to a friend after run 3?** Written *before* reading DESIGN.md:
- *Reactions:* "Enemies get a status from your hits: burning from fire, chilled/frozen from frost, shocked from lightning. Hit the same enemy with a second element and it blows up: fire+frost = Steam Burst, fire+lightning = Overload, frost+lightning = Superconduct. Purple (arcane) is a wild card that detonates any status: Combust on burning, Shatter on frozen, Arc Flash on shocked. Take cards that say 'Reacts with your X'."
- *Chain:* "A counter that goes up when you pop reactions back-to-back and gives some bonus, XP I think, maybe damage - I'm not sure how long I have between reactions, and I don't know what the 'x50!' means besides 'big'."
- *Fusions:* "If you take two skills up to rank 3 the game gives you a merged super-skill. I think any two work (the death screen says so) but some card hints show which skills go together. The merged skill shows up the next time you level up and uses up the two parents."

## Harness caveats
- The bot did all movement, aiming, dashing, skill firing and crystal shooting, so I could not judge whether the primer's pulse markers / "SHOOT IT" marker are actually findable by a human, how the controls feel, or whether the primer's timing (steps 2-5 flashed by within ~2 game seconds) is too fast. I also could not tell whether RMB/Q/E/R skills fire automatically or need the player; the bot cast them.
- Screenshots from `PT.shot` have no HUD; I used the browser pane screenshots at 800x600 for HUD checks, so HUD cramping may be partly a viewport-size effect.
- Difficulty comparisons are weak (bot skill `casual`); Cryomancer taking 0 damage is probably partly the bot's dashing and partly class design.
- In run 2 I switched to an auto-picker for ~4 level-ups and a few later relic picks to save calls (levels 22-24, including the Act II boss kill), so those picks are not in character; the earlier ~17 picks, all relics up to the Act II mid-point and the fusion decisions were deliberate.
- `PT.ui()` returned stale text from earlier runs; treat "tip"/ticker quotes as from the real DOM only when they matched the run's moment.
