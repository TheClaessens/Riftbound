# Mila — cozy casual · playtest round 1
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).**  It is genuinely gorgeous: a storybook meadow that turns into a desert and back, giant goblin swarms melting under purple vortexes, ice clouds and meteors, with a "number go up" chain counter that makes me feel like a god. But I only ever felt like a god with the Cryomancer (won Act III with zero damage taken, which was a little boring by the end), and my own hero disappears under the spell soup so I never quite felt like *I* was the one doing it; the other classes then died to the Act II boss out of nowhere. I'd come back tomorrow to try new class/skill combos, but I'd want the dull stretches (boss HP-sponge, long gaps between level-ups) trimmed.
Fun: 8/10 · Would play again tomorrow: yes (maybe) · Domain score: 8/10 (visuals 9, game feel 8, achievement 7, fairness 6)

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **The world is lovely.** Act I meadow, rivers, wildflowers, low-poly trees with soft shadows; Act II ochre badlands with bones and cracked earth; Act III back to a green ruined-bridge reach. Each biome shift reads instantly. [seen] (shots: `dev/shots/pt-mila-act1-crowd.png`, `pt-mila-act2-arrival.png`, `pt-mila-act3-arrival.png`)
- **Crowds are the star.** The Act I swarm with 150+ goblins pouring in, me nova-ing them into puffs, is exactly the "I'm a one-woman apocalypse" fantasy. [seen] (shot: `pt-mila-cryo-nova-crowd.png`, `pt-mila-arcanist-swarm.png`)
- **Spell effects have personality.** Meteor with its orange sky-beam and crater, the purple rune-circle vortex of Singularity, the cartoon white ice clouds of Frost Nova/Blizzard, lightning bolts from Static Field - all distinct, readable as "what that spell is". [seen] (shots: `pt-mila-swarm-peak.png`, `pt-mila-act2-fight.png`, `pt-mila-act3-fight.png`)
- **Heroes have charm.** Each class is cute and instantly distinguishable at a glance (red pyro, pale-blue ice mage, purple arcanist with hood, yellow-ringed Stormcaller with a pointy hat). [seen] (shots: `pt-mila-hero-closeup-lv2.png`, `pt-mila-cryo-hero.png`, `pt-mila-arcanist-hero.png`, `pt-mila-storm-hero.png`)
- **Chain counter / reaction toasts are addictive.** "Chain 10! / 25! / 50!" and the ever-climbing counter (1,611 at the end) are great juice. [read]/[data]
- **Card text with "Reacts with your fire: OVERLOAD" / "Fusion path: + Cinder Bolt -> Starfall" hints** let me pick cards by fantasy *and* nudged me toward combos without maths. [read]
- **Fusion moment**: seeing "Unlocks FUSION: Frostfire Comet!" on a card and then the actual fusion card appear at the next level-up is a nice little payoff. [read]
- **Primer is short and unobtrusive** (5 steps in ~16 s, with a real Steam Burst taught by actually triggering it). [read]
- **Sanctum shards feel fair**: even a death at 3:31 paid 62 shards, a win paid ~490; the class unlock banners on the end screen ("new riftbound: Cryomancer / Arcanist / Stormcaller") are a real reason to hit "Quest Again". [read]
- **Boss silhouettes and Act II boss readability**: Vashkar is clearly the biggest guy with a spiked mace/shield and the pink ring telegraphs are clear when the screen isn't a wall of FX. [seen] (shot: `pt-mila-boss2-closeup.png`)

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **I lose my own character in the effects.** In Act II/III peak chaos the hero is a tiny figure buried under purple rune rings, ice clouds and explosions; in several shots (`pt-mila-act2-chaos.png`, `pt-mila-boss3-fight.png`, `pt-mila-act3-fight.png`, `pt-mila-death3.png`) I had to hunt for her. For a power-fantasy player the avatar should always be findable. [seen] · Severity: major
2. **Deaths to the Act II boss feel like they come out of nowhere.** Arcanist and Stormcaller both died to Vashkar within seconds from healthy HP (Arcanist: 124 -> 5 HP in ~10 s, "boss flame ring" 73 + 36 dmg; Stormcaller: 104 -> 0 in ~28 s). With the screen filled with my own VFX and a red low-HP tint the ring/ground telegraphs get lost. [data] [seen] (shot: `pt-mila-lowhp-wide.png`) · Severity: major
3. **Huge difficulty gap between classes.** Cryomancer (138 HP, never took meaningful damage in Acts II-III, bosses died in 43-54 s) vs Pyromancer/Arcanist/Stormcaller dying at the Act I-II bosses. As a casual I'd conclude "ice mage is the easy mode" and feel bored by it. Also hurts sense of achievement: the dragon dying in 54 s felt like a non-event. [data] · Severity: major
4. **Level-up droughts then floods.** Cryomancer went from Lv 5 at 1:10 to Lv 6 at 2:13 (~60 s with ~300 kills), Arcanist Lv 13 -> 14 took ~2:45 of Act II, then boss start/death gave 3-5 levels in a few seconds (14 -> 17 in 4 s; 22 -> 23, 30 -> 32 in 1 s). The dopamine of the draft is the best part of the loop, so long empty stretches followed by 3 modals in a row feel uneven. [data] · Severity: minor/major
5. **Gorrak (Act I) is a boring HP sponge until he is suddenly lethal.** With the Cryomancer I spent 160 s of boss phase at 99%->87%->75% while the screen was full of trolls, then phase 2 hit. As Pyromancer he one-shot-ish me (80 melee dmg in 2 s) right at phase 2. The boss itself is not visually distinct from the regular trolls in the crowd (similar size/colour at a glance). [seen] [data] (shots: `pt-mila-boss1-intro.png`, `pt-mila-boss1-cryo.png`) · Severity: minor/major
6. **Fusions eat their parents and I wasn't told.** Frostfire Comet silently removed Fireball *and* Frost Nova from my loadout (skill list shrank from 6 to 5). I liked the result but it surprised me; the card says "Fuses Fireball + Frost Nova" without "(replaces both)". [read] · Severity: minor
7. **The Rift Primer ran only once and I never learned about Vashkar's fire resistance.** The announcement says "Fire will not hurt him much" for ~2 s on a banner, easy to miss; I then stacked four Meteor ranks. [read] · Severity: minor
8. **Low-HP red wash is very heavy.** At low HP the whole screen goes salmon-pink and the sepia death tint is similar; it hides pink telegraphs (which are also red!). [seen] (shots: `pt-mila-lowhp-wide.png`, `pt-mila-pyro-warboar-lowhp.png`) · Severity: minor
9. **Act I opening frame is empty** (first screenshot at t=0 shows only the orange cursor ring, no hero, no enemies). Probably harness timing but it is the very first thing I saw. [seen] (shot: `pt-mila-first-look.png`) · Severity: polish

## Domain deep-dive
**Would I come back tomorrow?** Yes, mostly for the visuals and the "what does this card combine into" curiosity (I saw 5/10 fusions and 9/18 relics, and want the rest). What would pull me back harder: a clear reason to retry Pyromancer, my starting class, which currently feels like the weakest at the start. What would put me off: dying to a boss I could not read.

**Best moment.** Run 2, about 2:10 in Act I as Cryomancer: 150+ goblins converge, I drop Frost Nova, the whole screen goes up in white puffs, chain hits x29 and then keeps going to x1,611 by the end. A close second: the Act II "rift shift" into the desert (`pt-mila-act2-arrival.png`), and Frostfire Comet's first cast.

**When did I get bored or annoyed?**
- Gorrak as Cryomancer: 160 s of boss phase, boss at 99% for the first 15 s and barely moving afterward, with nothing threatening me. Boring.
- Act II as Arcanist: long stretch without a level-up (13 -> 14 took ~2:45) while kills went from 1,300 to 2,400.
- Dying to Vashkar twice (Arcanist and Stormcaller) without being able to point to the thing that killed me: annoyed.
- Dying at 3:31 on the very first run to Gorrak: sting, softened by getting the Cryomancer unlock.

**Visuals / hero / world** (what a casual cares about): see Highlights. Rating 9/10. The only gripe is the avatar-in-effects problem and the red/sepia tints. Hero close-ups (`pt-mila-hero-closeup-lv2.png`, `pt-mila-cryo-hero.png`, `pt-mila-arcanist-hero.png`, `pt-mila-storm-hero.png`) are charming at the 10-unit crop, but at normal zoom the hero is only ~30 px tall.

**Power fantasy / juice.** Chain counter, reaction toasts, chain 10/25/50 banners, level-up modals with three cards, boss banners ("GORRAK, THE TROLL KING - The old king of the Thornwood wakes.") all land. I cannot judge sound. Hit-feel: enemies pop in cartoon puffs and ragdoll nicely, but at high density I cannot tell individual hits from each other. Level-up is a satisfying pause; the shot of the modal is in `pt-mila-levelup-moment-wide.png` (3D canvas only; the HUD text read fine in `PT.ui()`).

**Sense of achievement (shards, unlocks).** Decent: unlock banners (Cryomancer after run 1, Arcanist + Stormcaller + Rift Tier 1 after the run-2 victory) are clear rewards, shards of 60-500 per run feel proportional, and attunement costs (20/40/60... per rank) let me buy 3 things after a short run and ~12 after a win. The Sanctum's "might/vigor/insight/swiftcast/fortune/exile" boons are bland (numeric) but understandable. I would love one cosmetic/hero-flavour unlock too.

**Do deaths feel unfair or tedious?** Mixed. Fair: Run 5 (Pyro Act II): swarm + War Boar charge + projectiles, I could see all of it. Unfair-feeling: Run 3 and 4 Vashkar deaths and Run 1 Gorrak phase 2 (two-second 80 damage). Not tedious: runs are short enough that a death costs ~3-7 minutes and always pays shards. Run 2 victory at 13 min felt long (the last 3 minutes were passive).

**Bot-hands caveat for feel:** the bot never took damage as Cryomancer, which probably says more about the bot's dodging versus a Cryomancer's 138 HP than about a human's experience.

## Harness caveats
- The bot is the hands, so "I died to X" is weak evidence about difficulty; what killed me (damage by source) is the real data. The bot kept fighting adds instead of the boss (boss HP barely moved for ~60-70 s at the start of Gorrak with Cryomancer), which may exaggerate the "HP sponge" feeling.
- I used a preference-driven auto-picker for most level-ups in Runs 3-5 (to save time), choosing by fantasy (pretty/cool spells) rather than counters; that is how Mila would play but it led to Meteor spam against fire-resistant Vashkar.
- Screenshots have no HUD, so I could not judge HUD legibility, the level-up modal's visual design, or the shard counter animation; I only have `PT.ui()` text for those.
- No audio judged. Game runs faster than real time, so I could not feel the actual pacing of moment-to-moment input; "feel" notes come from stills.
- Only the Rift Tier 1 was tried (Run 4); I did not play tiers 2-5.
- I did not need [assisted] or [jumped] runs: Run 2 reached all three Acts and bosses naturally.
