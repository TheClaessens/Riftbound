# Playtest round 2 — v5 "Rift Obelisks & raid bosses" (addendum to BRIEF.md)

Read `design/playtest/BRIEF.md` first (harness, rules, report template). This file **overrides** it where they differ.
Build: v5 (pre-v5 backup `backup/riftbound-v4.2-preraid.zip`). URL: `http://localhost:8098/?unlockall` (Dex/Viktor/Rhea/Ilse) or `http://localhost:8098/` (Mila/Noor, fresh save).

## What is new (the only thing this round is about)
1. **Rift Obelisks.** At 0:50 of every act three obelisks rise: **Haste** (summon the boss now: skip the rest of the act's horde for a Windfall XP lump, shards and +1 relic choice), **Echo** (the boss comes back a second time as an Echo after you kill it: +10% damage taken all fight, pays a chest of rare Boss Relics + shards), **Wrath** (channel up to 3 times; each adds a pre-shown boss affix, harder boss, better chest + shards). You activate one by standing in its ring for 4 s while the horde converges on you. If you do nothing, the boss still comes at 3:00 as before.
2. **Raid-style boss encounters.** When the boss comes, an arena wall seals you in with it, the horde vanishes into XP, and the boss fight is an isolated encounter with its own score (audio is not evaluated), **3 phases** with **intermissions** (boss untargetable, an arena-scale pattern to survive), a **Stagger bar** (filled by elemental reactions, freezes, stuns, perfect dodges, Rift Crystal blasts on the boss — not by raw damage) that **Downs** the boss for a damage window, **Break Checks** (a named cast you must stagger before it finishes, e.g. Gorrak's War Horn), gold "go here" zones (cleanse pools, safe pockets, shelters), adds with a purpose (pods to kill before they ripen, banners that shield the boss, runners racing to heal him, a wyvern whose death leaves a lightning rod), and a soft enrage.
   * Gorrak (Act I): club slam, backhand cone (stand behind him), spore pods → sap pools, War Horn break check, Timberfall (dodge thrown trees), King's Wrath (dash through shockwaves).
   * Vashkar (Act II): axe in/out combo (out, then in), twin banners (shield generators), charges with lava, Molten Overswing break check (Axe Heat), Cinder Memory (marks that detonate where you walked), Molten Tide (the arena shrinks).
   * Aurelith (Act III): the Prism Ward colour tells you her next special; crystal pillars block beams (hide behind them); Prism Conduits; Prism Nova break check (stagger her or hide); a wyvern that leaves a lightning rod; the river floods the arena.

## New driver verbs and state
* `PT.obelisks()` → the three obelisks: state, distance, **cost/pay lines exactly as the in-game card shows**, Haste's net level estimate, Wrath's pre-rolled affix and heat.
* `PT.channel('haste' | 'echo' | 'wrath')` → your hands walk to that obelisk and stand in its ring; then `PT.play({ seconds: 20 })` until it reports `stopped: 'channelled'` (or check `PT.obelisks()`). For more Wrath heat call `PT.channel('wrath')` again. `PT.cancelChannel()` stops walking.
* `PT.play` now also stops at: `obelisks` (they rose), `phase` (boss phase / intermission change), `breakCheck` (a Break Check started), `channelled`. Use `PT.play({ seconds, stopAt: ['decision', 'end'] })` to ignore them.
* `state.boss` is a one-line readout of what the HUD shows: `"<name> 74% · phase 2/3 · DOWNED · stagger 40% · casting War Horn (break check: stagger 60) · objects: War Banner, …"`. `state.obelisks`, `state.stakes`, `state.debuffs` (spores/rooted/strain).
* `window.dmgBy` names boss hits by the move: `"Gorrak, the Troll King: backhand"`. `window.staggerGained` (by source), `window.bossDowned` (by reason), `window.breakChecks` (broken/failed).
* A Wrath Cache (heat 2+) arrives as a decision of `kind: 'wrathCache'` after the boss dies.
* `PT.end()` adds `obeliskShards` and `bossFights` (fight lengths).
* HUD text (`PT.ui()`) shows the boss frame (phase pips, STAGGER bar, cast bar, EXPOSED/DOWNED/SEALED chips, affixes), the obelisk approach card when you are near one, intermission strips and payout cards.

## Calibration (bot hands vs the v5 bosses, measured by the orchestrator)
* **expert** hands notice every ground telegraph (circles, cones, donuts, lines) and step out; they were hit by **none** of Vashkar's telegraphs in a full fight. They **cannot** time dashes through expanding shockwave rings (Gorrak's King's Wrath, Aurelith's crash) and cannot dodge Aurelith's sweeping beams or use pillar shelter deliberately — damage from those is a **bot limitation**, not proof of unfairness. Judge those by whether you could *see* them coming (screenshots, telegraph timing).
* **casual/average** hands notice fewer telegraphs, later. Deaths in boss fights are expected and are weak evidence.
* The bot reaches the Act I boss at about level 10–11 and the Act II boss at about 18–20. Typical bot boss fights: Gorrak ~70–90 s, Vashkar ~90–140 s.
* Without assist, bots rarely get past Act II (same as v4). Use `PT.start({ assist: 0.5 })` or `PT.jump(act, { boss: true })` to *see* Vashkar and Aurelith; tag those findings **[assisted]** / **[jumped]** and never base difficulty claims on them.
* The bot always aims at the boss. Killing pods/banners/conduits happens only when they're in the line of fire or hit by area spells — if you think a human would prioritise them, say so (that's a design-readability finding).
