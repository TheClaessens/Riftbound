# Viktor — hardcore buildcrafter · playtest round 2 (v5 Rift Obelisks & raid bosses)
**Verdict (in character).** The obelisk idea is a good risk/reward skeleton and the cards are mostly legible, but the numbers around it are lopsided: Wrath and Echo cost almost nothing to a build that already makes reactions (Stagger is trivial for it), while a build without reactions cannot meet Break Checks at all. Wrath heat 3 in Act II is the only lane that bit, and it killed me through one affix (Reverberating), not through the obelisk. Haste is a quiet trap and Echo a quiet freebie, and the boss-relic payout is thinner than the cards imply. I'd come back for the Echo/Wrath puzzle, but "mix reactions, ignore everything else" now looks like the best build for every lane.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe (yes if Stagger and the payouts get tuned) · Domain score (obelisk risk/reward): 5.5/10

## Highlights (what worked, keep these)
- **Cards are legible and honest about the numbers.** The Haste card says "Skips 130 s (about -5 levels)" and "Windfall (about +2 levels)", with a `levelsDelta` of -3. Act I Haste at L5 gave L8 at the boss (baseline L10 at 3:00), which matches. Echo and Wrath read as risk, payout, affix and heat. [read] [data]
- **Wrath's pre-shown affix ladder is the best buildcraft hook of the update.** I could read "Heat 2: Bulwark, Break Checks need 30 % more Stagger" before committing, and it ties directly to the Stagger economy. Run 3's War Horn needed 91 instead of 70 after Bulwark. [read] [data]
- **Boss relics as a pick-one-of-three** (Gorrak's Tusk, Aurelith's Scale, Vashkar's Brand) are exactly the cross-boss tailoring I want (take Vashkar's Brand before the fire-resistant Barrens boss). [read]
- **The Echo fight is a good "second life" beat.** "IT STIRS AGAIN", the Echo of Gorrak at 55 % HP and phase 2, +10 % strain. About 50-80 s extra, 15 shards, and a clean chest. [seen] (shot: `playtest/out/viktor-r2-v7-echo-rises.png`, `viktor-r2-v4-echo-rises.png`) [data]
- **Obelisk presentation is readable.** Gold ring plus tall stele for Haste, purple crystal cluster for Echo, and the ring sits on the ground clearly even in a mob. [seen] (shot: `viktor-r2-v5-haste-channel.png`, `viktor-r2-v6-echo-stuck.png`)
- **"Boosts 2 skills you own" tags on relics** are a great build-planning aid. [read]
- **The Wrath-3 Act III win (run 5) felt earned:** 21 HP left, with Second Wind spent in the fight. [data]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **Stagger is trivial for any reaction build and impossible for a non-reaction build.** Cryomancer with Ice Shard, Meteor and Frost Nova broke 7 of 7 Gorrak War Horns, usually in about 2 s, with 98 Stagger gained in the first 10 s of the fight (steam 53, superconduct 28). It was Downed for about 49 s of 172. Run 4's mono-frost Hailstorm failed 7 Break Checks and 3 more at Vashkar, so Molten Overswing "ground fire" cost about 115 HP and killed me. Same class, same tier, opposite outcomes, purely from picking one more element. This pushes every build toward "fire + frost + storm, keep reacting", and mono-element is a trap. [data] [seen] · Severity: **major** (kills build variety).
2. **Dying forfeits every obelisk payout, and the payout is thin.** Runs 1 and 3 (Wrath 3 / Echo + Wrath 2) paid 0 shards, 0 chest, 0 cache, and OS = 0. Run 1 died at 21 % boss HP after a long fight. The whole stake is lost and nothing partial remains. For a "risk" lane that is acceptable, but nothing tells you on the card that a death forfeits it. Meanwhile the rewards are small: Echo 15 shards, Wrath 2 12 shards, Wrath 3 30 shards in Act I (60 in Act III), Haste about 29. A run earns 180-470 shards. [data] · Severity: **major**.
3. **"+1 relic choice in the boss chest" is misleading.** On the Haste and Wrath cards it reads like an extra pick. It is one extra card on a normal pick-one chest (4 options instead of 3). I only noticed when the chest showed 4 cards. Wrath heat 3 says "A Boss Relic is guaranteed" but that gives one boss relic among four cards, which I can simply not take. Echo is the only lane that gives a pick from a dedicated boss-relic set. [read] [data] · Severity: **major** (legibility).
4. **Wrath 3 Reverberating is a single-affix death trap that the Stagger and mobility kit can't answer.** Run 1 took about 240 damage from "reverberation" in one Vashkar fight, including 45 during an intermission when the boss was untargetable, and finished at 21 % boss HP. The affix text says "Dash through it", but with 3 dashes and 2-3 s cooldowns it is near-constant. Bot hands can't dash through rings (known limitation), so a human may do better, but I could see these ring waves only as a faint crack in shots. [data] [seen] (shot: `viktor-r2-v1-vashkar-reverb.png`) · Severity: **major** [inferred for the human case].
5. **Haste is the weakest lane, and it forfeits things the card doesn't price.** At Act I, level 5, Haste cost 2 levels net at the boss and a fight at L8, with only about 29 shards back. At Act II it is -3 levels, and by Act III -4 (-6 +2). Forfeiting "Elite Surge" also forfeits the Relic Bearer (the only mid-act relic source, roughly 1 relic per act). The card prints "Forfeits: Elite Surge, Swarm" but does not say that a relic goes with it. I took Haste twice and was never glad. Bounties scale with the act (33, 49, 65) but so does the loss. [data] · Severity: minor-major.
6. **Boss relics are a pool of three, always the same three.** Runs 4 and 7 (and the Act I chests in 5/6) all offered Gorrak's Tusk, Aurelith's Scale and Vashkar's Brand, in a different order. It's a fine first pick, but a repeat buildcrafter will know it by run 3. [data] · Severity: minor.
7. **Mid-fight modal relic choice.** The Relic Bearer's relic (from the elite surge) interrupted Gorrak's fight in run 4 at 3:02, one second after the boss spawned. It is a pause, not a loss, but it breaks the "isolated encounter" feel. [data] · Severity: polish.

## Domain deep-dive
**Are the trades legible before you commit?** Mostly yes. `PT.obelisks()` prints cost, pay, `levelsDelta` and the next affix. What I got wrong or couldn't see: (a) "+1 relic choice" reads as an extra pick (it is a 4th card); (b) Haste forfeits the Relic Bearer; (c) a death forfeits everything; (d) Wrath keeps heating while you stand there; (e) the shard numbers on the card differ a few points from what is paid. I'd want a "total expected payout" line.

**Is any obelisk a no-brainer or a never-pick?** Wrath heat 1 and 2 are a free pick for a reaction build: in run 7, Echo + Wrath 2 killed Gorrak and the Echo with 155 of 170 HP left, paid 27 shards, a 4-card chest, a 3-card boss-relic chest and a Cache. Echo alone was also nearly free (run 4: +15 shards and a boss relic for about 80 s and some HP). Haste is the closest to a never-pick: -2 to -4 levels for 29-65 shards and one extra card, and your first Elite Surge relic is gone. Wrath 3 is the only truly risky lane: Run 1 died, run 5's Act III nearly did, run 6 survived at 55 HP and run 3's Arcanist (Echo + Wrath 2) died. It also pays best (30 in Act I, 60 in Act III) but still only about 13 % of a run's shards. I'd call Haste the never-pick and Wrath 1-2 + Echo the no-brainer for a reaction build.

**Do Boss Relics and the Wrath Cache feel worth it?** Boss relics: yes, as a choice, no as a pool (3 relics). Each is good (Tusk gives stun, hence Stagger; Brand removes Barrens fire resistance; Scale gives guaranteed echoes), but I saw the same three every time. The Wrath Cache (+1 skill rank from 3 options, no rerolls) is a decent but small payout, on par with one level-up. Heat 3 swaps it for a boss-relic card, which is not an obviously better prize.

**Does channelling under Rift Pull create a fun moment or a tax?** It depends on power, both ways. At L31+ with Frost Nova r5 and Meteor r5 (run 5, Act III), Wrath 1 to 3 completed in 16 s while my HP climbed (186 to 200); it was a tax-free cash-in. At L7-L10 with 3-4 skills (runs 2, 4, 5 Act II), the pull kept the bot out of the ring and cost 100+ HP (182 down to 59 in 20 s at Act III, L23) with nothing gained, and the bot bounced off the ring. That is the fun-moment case: a weak build gets punished and has to commit. My complaint is the strength curve is steep: a strong build ignores it, a weak build can't do it at all, and there's little middle. Also the first 20 s after the obelisks rise is the safest time to channel (small horde), which the card never says.

**Does any build trivialise the Stagger bar?** Yes. Any build with two or more elements and a few reactions. Cryomancer Ice Shard + Meteor + Frost Nova + Chain Lightning averaged about 98 Stagger in 10 s on Gorrak, 5 s Break Checks were broken in about 2 s, and the boss was Downed for roughly 28 % of the fight (about 49 of 172 s). Superconduct and steam do most of it (steam 226 + superconduct 48 in 50 s). Opposite problem: mono-frost fails Break Checks outright (7 of 7 failed in run 4). Bulwark (+30 % Break Check cost, Downed -1 s) did not matter to the reaction build and ended a non-reaction one.

**How many different builds exist?** In this round I effectively found two: "reaction spam with 3+ elements" (wins, any class) and "pure element" (loses to Break Checks). The Arcanist (Spirit Orbs r4 + Arcane Bolt + Fireball) killed Gorrak's phase 1 in about 10 s but had too little defence; the Stormcaller (Spark + Chain Lightning) was thin. Fusions (Hailstorm, Storm Halo) are still a 2-for-1 that thins the kit at a time when the boss wants variety (run 4 had one skill at L10).

**What would keep me playing 20 hours?** A real Wrath ladder at tier 3-5 where the third affix is a decision rather than a coin-flip, more boss relics, and a reason to take Haste (fast-clear tier shards or speed-run rewards). Also payouts that survive death.

## Harness caveats
- **The bot can't use obelisks reliably.** After about 1:10 of an act the horde makes the hands hover 8-16 units away for a minute or more. A human can walk into a ring on purpose; my "Haste reachable" and "Echo reachable" findings are bot-limited. In a few runs the ring was across a river and the bot couldn't or wouldn't cross.
- **The bot overshoots Wrath heat** because it stays in the ring. I had to stop it manually to get heat 2.
- **Reverberation, King's Wrath and Aurelith's beams** are bot limitations on timing (per ROUND.md); I can't judge their human fairness. Run 3's Arcanist death (33 % boss HP, King's Wrath) is weak evidence.
- **My drafting was uneven.** Runs 2, 3 and 4 had poor skill selection (I chased fusion paths and auto-picked rank-ups), so their low power is partly mine. I also auto-picked some relics in run 1 (Second Wind) and run 2 (Stormglass Boots).
- **Only one run reached Act III**, and the victory screen hid any chest. Haste at Act III and Echo at Act III were not played. Tier 2 was one partial run; I can't speak to Tier 3-5 scaling.
- Several decisions I took were in the same order for several runs, so the "boss relic pool" claim rests on 4 chests, not a lot.
