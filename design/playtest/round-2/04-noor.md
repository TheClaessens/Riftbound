# Noor — genre newcomer · playtest round 2
**Verdict (in character, 2–3 sentences).** The obelisk cards are the best teaching in the game: I could read what Echo and Haste cost and paid and make a choice without any help. The boss fight is another story: the screen tells me *that* things are happening (boss frame, chips, strips), but never explains the words (Stagger, Downed, Winded, Sealed, Strain, Windfall), so I only "learned" Stagger by accident, because my reactions happened to fill it. I died twice to the same unreadable-for-a-newcomer thing, King's Wrath, and the death screen gave advice about a different attack.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: yes (the Haste win was a genuine rush) · Domain score (learnability): 5/10

All screenshots in this report were taken from the browser pane with the HUD on (the harness `PT.shot` is canvas only); the two `PT.shot` files are `playtest/out/noor-r2-obelisks-rise.png` and `playtest/out/noor-r2-gorrak-intermission.png`.

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **The obelisk approach card is clear and honest.** "SUMMON NOW / Obelisk of Haste / ≈ −2 LEVELS", then COST and PAY lines, then "HOLD THE RING TO CHANNEL". The one-glance net-level chip is great. I understood Echo ("the boss comes twice, +10% damage taken, pays 3 relics to choose from + 15 shards") and Haste ("skip time, get a lump of XP and a bonus relic") immediately. [seen] [read] (shots: Echo card at 1:20, Haste card at 1:04, via pane screenshot)
- **The tip when the obelisks rise** ("Obelisks let you call the boss your way. Haste summons it now, Echo brings it twice, Wrath adds affixes. Stand in a ring for 4s to arm one. Doing nothing is fine.") is the right length and tone. "Doing nothing is fine" removes the fear. [read]
- **The Break Check strip is the best boss-UI piece.** "WAR HORN — BREAK IT — STAGGER 60 — 5.0" with a notch on the Stagger bar at 60, then a big "BROKEN!" / "FAILED" banner. You do not need to know the word "stagger" to see "a bar must reach a notch before a countdown ends." [seen]
- **The boss frame** (name, phase pips under the HP bar, a Stagger bar underneath, chips for DOWNED / WINDED / SEALED, affix chips for Wrath) fits in one place and does not hide the arena much. The big pale "+15 +15" numbers in the stagger bar show *what* fills it. [seen]
- **Red telegraph = danger** is taught by the boss-arrival tip ("Every attack is telegraphed in red. Step out, or dash through it.") and then holds: slam circles, Timberfall lanes, the King's Wrath ring and Vashkar's rings are all red. [seen] [read]
- **Edge markers** ("Haste 28m / Echo 22m / Wrath 17m", later "War Banner 34m") tell me where things are without any text. [seen]
- **Haste feels great as a newcomer's first win.** Windfall banner, boss in 3 seconds, a fight I could finish at level 7 with full HP and a 4-relic chest. It made me feel clever. [seen] [data] (time 1:21, lowest HP 28/108)
- **Death-screen "last moments" list** ("2 hits in 2.3 s, King's Wrath −17 −17") makes it plain *what* killed me even though the generic hint is off (see pain point 1). [read]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **The "next time" hint on the death screen is about the wrong attack.** Both times I died to *King's Wrath* (the expanding ring in Intermission II), the game said: "next time Gorrak slams and rains meteors inside red circles. Leave the circle before it fills." That is a different attack (club slam), and "leave the circle" is the *opposite* of the right answer for an expanding ring (dash through it, per the primer tip, which was never shown for this attack). A newcomer will leave the death screen believing the wrong lesson. 2 of 2 deaths. [read] [data] · Severity: major
2. **King's Wrath is not taught at all, and the intermission hides the one thing the player must do.** Intermission strip says "INTERMISSION · II · KING'S WRATH 1.4" and a big pink ring grows. Nothing says "dash through it" for this one (the only "dash through" text I saw was on the generic boss tip and on Wrath I's "Reverberating" announcement). I took 33 then 17 + 17 and died from 14-29 HP, both times at 3:57. A newcomer will try to run *away* from a ring that is bigger than the screen. [seen] [read] (shot: `playtest/out/noor-r2-gorrak-intermission.png` is Timberfall; King's Wrath screenshot in pane at 3:55) · Severity: major
3. **The boss fight uses six words the game never defines.** DOWNED, WINDED, SEALED, RECOVERING, STAGGER, STRAIN (debuff chip after Echo). I got 3 different chips (DOWNED 4 s, WINDED 3 s, SEALED) with no hint that DOWNED = "hit it now" and WINDED = a shorter window after a phase change, and that SEALED = untargetable during an intermission. The chip colours look alike (grey pills). I inferred "DOWNED is good" from the damage numbers, not from the UI. [seen] [read] · Severity: major
4. **Nothing connects "reactions" to "Stagger".** The in-game tip for a Break Check says "Fill the Stagger bar past the marker", but not *how*. In every run my reactions filled it by accident (`staggerGained` combust/steam/overload 60-135 per fight). A newcomer who has not built reaction skills (or just taps one skill) would have a Stagger bar that never fills and a boss that never goes down. The primer teaches reactions for XP/Chain, and the boss teaches Stagger, but nothing says they are the same thing. [data] [read] [inferred] · Severity: major
5. **Gold vs red is only half-legible.** Red = danger is solid. But "gold = go here" is not taught: on Gorrak I saw (a) gold-white discs on the ground (sap/cleanse pools, I assume) and (b) green-yellow glowing pods in red rings (spore pods, red ring = danger or target?) and (c) yellow dashed rings that are just Rift Crystal rings in the Barrens. Three yellow-ish things with three different meanings, none labelled. A pod inside a red ring looks like "get out" and also "kill me". In Vashkar's fight a small cyan ring appeared at my feet (Cinder Memory mark? banner aura?) with no label. [seen] · Severity: major
6. **Obelisk wording that a newcomer has to guess.** "Forfeits: Elite Surge, Swarm" (I had not seen Swarm yet in run 3: I'd only seen it announced in run 1), "Windfall", "Strain", "Boss Relics", "Wrath heat", "pre-shown affix". Also: **Haste is also the name of a passive** (−7% cooldowns) that showed up as a level-up card in the same minute as the Haste obelisk (run 1, level 4 and 7). I read the card "Haste -7% cooldowns" and thought it was related. [read] · Severity: minor
7. **Wrath's channel is almost invisible, and I accidentally went to heat 2.** I told the bot to channel Wrath once; the text popup said "CHANNELLING 20% → 65%" (no cost/pay on that card), and shortly after the game announced "WRATH II: Rift Storm" without my asking. The cost line on the approach card ("Packs converge on you while you channel", "Boss HP +15%") disappears the moment you're in the ring, which is exactly when you want to re-check it. I did not realise that I had stacked two affixes until the boss frame showed two chips. [seen] [read] · Severity: minor
8. **Intermission is a fine idea, but the screen is stacked with overlays.** At 3:30 I had "PHASE II SPORE ROT" in the middle, the intermission strip, a break-check strip, a tip card at the left, two relic toasts and a reaction feed all at once (see Gorrak P3 shot at 2:20 in run 3). The "PHASE" banner overlapped the intermission strip and the Break Check strip. [seen] · Severity: minor
9. **No way to heal in the arena, and nothing tells you.** All HP lost in the horde and in the fight stays lost (I entered phase 2 at 56/98, phase 3 at ~25-30). Because the horde "vanishes into XP" there are no health pickups. A newcomer will not know that the HP bar at the start of the boss is the budget for the whole fight. [data] [inferred] · Severity: minor
10. **Left-over HUD numbers are mysterious.** Bottom-left of the screen shows "99996" (a purple icon) and "14" (red icon) with no label while I fight; I could not tell what they were (kills left? rift energy?). Not new in v5, but it sits right next to the boss fight. [seen] · Severity: polish

## Domain deep-dive
**Do I understand what the obelisks do from the in-game card?** Yes, Echo and Haste fully, Wrath mostly. Echo: "The boss comes twice. Cost: +10% damage taken for the fight, the boss rises again as an Echo (55% HP, starts at Phase 2). Pay: pick 1 of 3 Boss Relics + 15 shards." Haste: "Summon now, ≈ −2 levels: skips ~116 s, forfeits Elite Surge and Swarm; pays +247 XP, +29 shards, +1 relic choice." Wrath card shows "Boss affix: Frenzied (Attacks come 20% faster.) / Packs converge on you while you channel" and "Heat 1: 5 shards in total". All three are readable. Terms I had to guess: Strain, Windfall, Elite Surge, Swarm (before I'd seen them), Boss Relics, "Heat". I did **not** catch the Wrath card before it started channelling because the hands walk fast. The tip "Doing nothing is fine" matters.

**Which would I pick as a newcomer?** Haste on run 3: it's the only one where the card gives a number ("≈ −2 LEVELS") that I can relate to. Echo reads "scary but fair". Wrath I took on run 2 because I wanted to see an affix; I did not understand that heat 2 was already stacked. The boss on Wrath 2 was a lot meaner (Rift Storm + Reverberating shockwaves) and I wasn't able to attribute my damage to the affixes because the combat log only says "Gorrak: timber / backhand / kings wrath".

**Stagger bar / Break Check / Downed / intermission from the HUD and tips alone?**
- *Break Check:* yes. The countdown strip with the stagger notch is self-explanatory, and the BROKEN / FAILED banner gives feedback. 5 of 7 broke in my runs without my doing anything. [data]
- *Stagger bar:* half. I can see a bar fill with +15 pops and a notch. I could not tell *what* fills it until I read `staggerGained` in the harness (reactions). The primer teaches reactions for Chain, never for the boss. A player who doesn't build around reactions will see a bar that doesn't move.
- *Downed:* I learned it by seeing "DOWNED" with a timer, a big damage number spike, and the boss falling over. But the chip is a grey pill and "WINDED" looks the same, with a shorter timer. I would have liked a "hit it now!" cue.
- *Intermission:* I understood "the boss goes away and the arena does a pattern" from the strip ("INTERMISSION · I · TIMBERFALL 5.2"), and "SEALED" on the boss frame. But I did not know I couldn't damage him (the "SEALED" chip didn't say so) and spent the whole time shooting at nothing in the first intermission. For King's Wrath the strip gives no instruction. In Timberfall the red lanes are clear and I only got hit once for 13-25.

**Gold zones vs red zones: is "go here" vs "get out" obvious?** Red = get out: yes, clearly, helped by the arrival tip. Gold = go here: no. I saw gold-white discs (Gorrak's sap pools / cleanse pools?), yellow pods in red rings, yellow dashed Rift Crystal rings in Act II, and a gold relic marker. None carried a word. I never *intentionally* stood in a gold zone; a bot doesn't. A human newcomer would mostly ignore them or fear them.

**What confused me most?** (1) That a Troll King's "King's Wrath" ring kills you from 30 HP and the death screen blamed a slam. (2) What fills Stagger. (3) The yellow things. (4) That the boss frame disappeared during the arrival title card in run 1 so I did not know which bar was his HP for ~10 s. (5) The stacked banners during phase changes.

**After 4 runs could I explain this to a friend?** "Three pillars appear at 50 s: take Haste to skip ahead, Echo for a harder double boss, Wrath to make the boss meaner for better loot. The boss has three phases; between them he vanishes and the arena tries to hurt you. If a bar fills (use reactions) he falls over and you can hit him for a few seconds. When he starts a named cast, fill the bar to the notch before the timer ends. Red is danger; I'm not sure what gold means." That is most of it. The Chain/reactions/fusions bit from round 1 is separate from this round.

## Harness caveats
- Casual hands made every King's Wrath and every Vashkar hit; I cannot say whether a human who reads the ring would survive. Dash timing through the expanding ring is the known bot limitation (ROUND.md), so the deaths themselves are weak evidence. The *missing instruction* is strong evidence.
- The hands walk straight to a ring when I `PT.channel`, so I saw each approach card only because I stepped the game 1-2 s at a time. A human would also have time to read it.
- `PT.ui()` carries stale text; most of my "reading" used pane screenshots of the live HUD. rAF is frozen, so overlay fade-outs (boss title, PHASE banners) linger longer than they would for a human and make the screen look more cluttered than it really is.
- Run 3 (the Haste win) and run 4 (jump) share a save with other testers on the same origin (localhost:8098), so shard totals may include other personas' activity (my own `PT.end()` numbers for runs 1 and 2: 53 and 51; run 4: 32).
- Run 4 was [jumped] and [assisted] and used auto-drafted cards; Vashkar observations are readability only.
- I never reached Aurelith or an Echo fight, and never saw a Wrath Cache or the Echo chest (died first / won with Haste). The Haste chest showed 4 relic options and "Haste Bounty +27 shards".
