# Mila — cozy casual · playtest round 2 (v5 obelisks and raid bosses)
**Verdict (in character).** The boss arenas are gorgeous. The thorn wall in the Thornwood, the lava-rock ring and tethered banners in the Barrens, and the crystal wall with the dragon in the Reach all made me go "oh!". But the "event" moment is smaller than it should be: the seal, the horde melting into XP and the boss landing mostly slid past me, and I died to boss hits I never saw coming more than once. I would try again tomorrow, mostly to see Aurelith properly. I would not enjoy being hit for a third of my health by an attack I can't tell is happening.
Fun: 7/10 · Would play again tomorrow: maybe (yes if the first 3 minutes stop killing me) · Domain score (epic feel and frustration): 6.5/10

Hands: `casual`, class Pyromancer throughout, fresh save, primer on (it played once, in run 1). Runs 3 and 4 used `assist: 0.5` and run 4 ended in a `jump` to Act III. Those are tagged **[assisted]** and **[jumped]** and are not evidence of difficulty.

## Highlights (what worked — keep these)
- **The arenas are stunning and each boss has its own look.** Gorrak's ring is a living thorn-and-vine wall, Vashkar's a lava-rock ring with teal banner tethers, and Aurelith's a glowing crystal barrier with prism pillars. [seen] (shots: `playtest/out/mila-r2-natural-arrival-6.png`, `mila-r2-vashkar-arrive-5.png`, `mila-r2-aurelith-arrive.png`)
- **The seal moment is a great beat.** The horde melts into a swirl of blue XP and the field goes quiet and empty, with only the crystals left. It sells "now it's just you and him". [seen] (shot: `mila-r2-natural-arrival-5.png`)
- **The obelisks themselves look cool and read as "optional dares".** The black spiky Wrath obelisk with its orange ring looks properly menacing, and the Haste obelisk has a golden ring. Distance chips ("Echo 39m / Wrath 32m") on the HUD help you find them. [seen] (shots: `mila-r2-obelisk-approach.png`, `mila-r2-wrath-ring.png`)
- **The obelisk card text is plain English.** "Skips 130s of this act", "Windfall +267 XP", "Boss affix: Rift Storm", "Doing nothing is fine." The tip line is exactly the reassurance a cozy player needs. [read]
- **Telegraphs are big and obvious when you can see them.** Red circles and lanes (Gorrak's slam, Vashkar's charge line) are huge and clear, and the Aurelith ground markers are easy to read. [seen] (shots: `mila-r2-boss-landing.png`, `mila-r2-vashkar-intermission.png`)
- **Rift Mend plus "The Echo rises" is a lovely mini-cutscene.** "+20% health · dash restored · the field is clear" is the best moment of the whole boss design. It gives you a breather and tells you what is coming. [read]
- **The death screen is great for a casual.** "Slain by Gorrak backhand 29", a damage breakdown, "The last moments: one hit −29", and a "next time…" tip. I always understood who killed me. [read]
- **Echo and Wrath were both easy to arm once I got to them, and they paid visibly.** The payout line reads "+45 shards (Echo +15; Wrath 3 +30)", with three Boss Relics to choose from. [data]
- **Level-ups, reactions and chain popups feel juicy.** A "Chain ×224 / ×504" pile-up is very satisfying. [read]

## Pain points (ranked, worst first)
1. **I couldn't tell which of Gorrak's hits would hurt, and the worst one wasn't telegraphed as a hit.** In run 2 two "Gorrak: backhand" hits (29 each) killed me from 62 and 42 HP. The death-screen tip said "Gorrak slams and rains meteors inside red circles" and never mentioned the backhand. [data] [read] · Severity: major
2. **Casual hands can't hold an obelisk ring.** Run 1 never completed Echo (the horde converged and killed me), run 2 needed several re-issued `PT.channel` calls to finish Haste, and run 3 never completed Wrath (HP fell to 20/64). It worked only in quieter moments or with assist. A real casual would probably give up. The obelisk is scary to try, not inviting. [data] [seen] · Severity: major
3. **The boss "landing" arrives while a level-up modal is open.** On the natural 3:00 arrival and after Haste, Windfall level-ups queue up (2 to 3 modals back to back). The boss landed under the modals and I only saw the arena after. "THE BOSS COMES IN 3 SECONDS" never got its dramatic moment. [data] [read] · Severity: major for the "event" feel
4. **Bosses are small and the hit-flash is a white blob.** Gorrak is only a few tiles across and often white-flashes, so on screen it looks like a ghost. Aurelith has a dramatic entrance but is not large, and she is tinted orange by a fire overlay while I fight her. Epic feel comes from the arena, not the boss silhouette. [seen] (shots: `mila-r2-gorrak-2.png`, `mila-r2-boss-landing.png`, `mila-r2-aurelith-novacast.png`) · Severity: minor/polish
5. **Break Checks went unexplained for ~two casts, and I failed War Horn 3 times in a row on Gorrak.** The tip "Fill the Stagger bar past the marker before the cast ends" appears only on the first one. A cozy player doesn't know *how* to fill Stagger beyond "reactions" (it reads as jargon). [read] [data] · Severity: minor
6. **Stale text leaks in the HUD text between runs and fights.** After a new run `PT.ui()` still shows "Gorrak, The Troll King Falls", "BOSS SLAIN FELLED IN 0:42" and "STAKES … HASTE skipped 1:28" from earlier runs. These may be hidden DOM nodes, but it was present in text a screen reader would read. [read] · Severity: polish (could be harness-only)
7. **Haste's cost is hard to compare against its payout.** It says "about -4 levels" under cost, "+2 levels" in Windfall, and a net "≈ −2 LEVELS" in the header. A casual just wants one sentence: "you will be about 2 levels weaker, but get 33 shards and a relic." [read] · Severity: minor
8. **Wrath Cache is underwhelming compared to what it cost.** At heat 3 I'd taken three boss affixes (Bulwark, Reverberating, Rift Storm) for +1 rank on a random skill. Echo at least gave a Boss Relic choice. [data] · Severity: minor
9. **Act I is lethal to a casual pre-boss (run 1, 2:12).** 47 of 54 damage came from Goblin Shamans; I died to chip damage with no boss in sight and 41 shards. It makes people quit before ever seeing the new content. [data] · Severity: major for retention (this overlaps with round 1)

- `PT.channel` needed re-issuing after every level-up or relic modal — walking stops when a decision opens. That's fine for a human, but it means long Windfall queues cancel the dash to the obelisk. [data]
- Death-screen tips are generic and not tied to the actual killer: Vashkar killed me with "contact" and the tip talked about a charge line and flame rings; Aurelith killed me with a Nova and the tip said "sweeps beams". [read]
- At Vashkar the banners are wired with a teal beam to the boss. I never understood from the HUD that they were shield generators. "objects: War Banner, War Banner" is data-ese. [read]
- `PT.end()` `obeliskShards` showed 0 in the Haste run (the payout seems to need the boss kill), and Haste's "Haste Bounty +33" is never confirmed in the end report because I died first. Minor, but a dead run forfeits it. [data]
- Heat 3 Wrath took only 17 s of game time to arm all three channels (1:00 to 1:17), so the "dare" part is shorter than the card suggests. [data]

## Domain deep-dive (round-2 questions)
**Does the boss arrival feel like an event (the seal, the wall, the boss landing)?** Partly. The wall and the horde-melting-into-XP are the best part, and the arena shape is a clear "something big is happening" cue. The weak spot is timing: the boss often lands while I'm picking cards, and the intro banner ("THE HEART STIRS / THE BOSS COMES IN 3 SECONDS") disappears behind modals. When I played it with no queued level-ups (run 3, natural 3:00) the sequence was much better. [seen] [read] (shots: `mila-r2-natural-arrival-4/5/6.png`, `mila-r2-boss-landing.png`)

**Do the bosses feel epic and distinct?** The arenas and mechanics are clearly distinct: Gorrak is thorns, spore pods and swung trees, Vashkar is lava, banners and a charge line, and Aurelith is crystal pillars with a colour-coded ward. The boss models themselves are smaller than I'd want, and in fights they are often a flash of white. Aurelith looks the most epic, with the crystal ring closing and the beam through the arena. Fight length was fine for the "event" (Gorrak 44 s on assist; 96 s with Wrath 3; 132 s for the Echo) but the Echo felt like a long second fight. [seen] [data]

**Are the obelisks inviting or scary?** Scary. The visuals invite me (they look like treasure shrines), and the card text is honest. But the "stand in a ring for 4 s while the horde converges" part is a real danger to a casual's hands — in run 1 it was why I died, and in run 3 Wrath nearly did (20/64 HP). When it worked (Haste in run 2, Echo in run 3 and 4, Wrath 3 in run 4) it was in a lull or with assist. A casual will pick **Haste** (shortest, clear payout) or nothing. **Echo** sounds epic but the "+10% damage taken" worries me. **Wrath** looks the most dangerous and is the most paid. The "doing nothing is fine" tip is good and I'd keep it. [data] [seen] [read]

**When I died, did I understand why, and did I want to try again?** Yes for who: the death screen always named the boss and move. No for how to avoid it: "backhand" had no warning, Vashkar's hit was labelled "contact", and the tips talked about other moves. My urge to retry was strong after run 2 and run 3 (I was close to killing Gorrak and Vashkar), and weak after run 1 (died to trash in 2 minutes with nothing to show for it). The class unlocks on the death screen ("new riftbound: Arcanist") soften losses a lot. [read] [data]

**Best moment.** Run 3, the natural 3:00: the horde bursts into XP, the thorn wall closes, I'm alone with Gorrak, and I killed him in 44 s with a huge Chain ×98 reaction pile-up. A close second: the "ECHO OF GORRAK — It returns. Weaker, faster, angrier." banner after Rift Mend. [seen] [read]

**Worst moment.** Run 1: I walked toward the Echo obelisk, the horde converged, and I died to Goblin Shamans at 2:12 with no boss and no obelisk reward. Second worst: losing 29 HP to an unexplained backhand twice in a row in run 2. [data]

**Would I come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The art and the arenas are a strong pull, and I want to see Aurelith fairly. I'd stop if the first 3 minutes keep killing me and the new content stays gated behind that.

## Harness caveats
- **Casual bot hands** don't dodge much and don't hold the obelisk ring reliably, so my "obelisk is scary" finding is partly the bot. A human could kite out of the horde first. Run 1's death is a bot-skill outcome and weak evidence of unfairness. [inferred]
- Runs 3 and 4 used `assist: 0.5` and run 4 was `jump`ed to Act III with 73 max HP (accidental Glass Sigil). Aurelith's difficulty here is not evidence of balance. I only saw about 25 s of that fight.
- My auto-picker chose relics by "first option", which put **Glass Sigil** into runs 3 and 4. Mila would pick by name too, but this skewed my HP.
- The bot always aims at the boss, so I never saw adds (pods, banners, conduits) handled by purpose.
- Screenshots have no HUD, so I couldn't judge the boss frame (stagger bar, phase pips, cast bar) visually, only through `PT.ui()` text. The overlays in the screenshots (orange tint) may be low-HP or ward effects. I'm not sure.
- Audio (the boss score, horn, impact) is not evaluated, and it is a big part of "epic".
